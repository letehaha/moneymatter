**Add Expense** logs a cash or card expense in a few taps, without opening the MoneyMatter iPhone app. It asks for the account, the amount and the category, and that's it. You add the rest later in the app.

## Before you start

- **Your phone must be unlocked.**
- **Open MoneyMatter once while signed in.** That loads your accounts and categories onto the phone. Until then, the shortcut says "Open MoneyMatter to load your accounts and categories, then try again."

## Start it

Use whichever is closest:

- **Siri**: say "Add expense in MoneyMatter", "Log expense in MoneyMatter" or "MoneyMatter expense".
- **Spotlight**: swipe down on the Home Screen and search for **Add Expense**.
- **Action button** (iPhone 15 Pro and later): open the **Settings** app → **Action Button**, swipe to **Shortcut**, then choose **MoneyMatter** → **Add Expense**.
- **Shortcuts app**: in **Library**, open **MoneyMatter** and tap **Add Expense**.

## Log an expense

<Steps>

1. **Account**: pick the account you paid from.
2. **Amount**: type the amount. It's in the currency of the account you picked.
3. **Category**: pick one. **Which category?** lists up to 10 categories you used recently first, then all your categories A to Z, so a recent category shows up twice.

</Steps>

Shortcuts confirms the saved expense. It's created in MoneyMatter the next time you open the app. Open it to add a payee, note or anything else.

- **No AI and no internet needed.** You can log an expense offline. It's sent when you next open the app.
- **Bank-connected accounts** get a planned transaction. It's confirmed when your bank sync reports the same amount. If the bank never reports it, delete the planned transaction.
- **Manual accounts** get a regular transaction.

## Choose what the shortcut lists

By default, **Add Expense** only offers your manual accounts, because payments on bank-connected accounts arrive through sync. You can change this, and hide categories you never use for quick expenses.

- **In the web app**: open **Settings** → **General** and find **iPhone Add Expense**.
- **On iPhone**: tap your avatar → **Settings** → **General** → **Add Expense shortcut**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show bank-connected accounts** | Off by default. Turn it on to list bank-connected accounts too. |
| **Hidden categories** | Checked categories don't appear in the shortcut's category list. Hiding a category hides its subcategories too. |

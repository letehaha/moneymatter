The MoneyMatter iPhone app adds two actions to Apple's **Shortcuts** app. Put one in an automation, and your iPhone logs a payment every time you tap your card, your bank texts you or your bank's app sends a notification. You set it up once per card or bank.

| You pay with | Automation trigger | MoneyMatter action |
|---|---|---|
| A card in Apple Wallet, in a shop | **Wallet** | **Log Wallet Payment** |
| Any card, and your bank texts you | **Message** | **Log Payment Message** |
| Any card, and your bank app notifies you | **Notification** | **Log Payment Message** |

The steps follow iOS 27. The small illustrations are drawn to match Shortcuts, so colours and spacing differ a little from your phone.

## Before you start <Badge text="Plus" variant="tip" />

- **Plus plan.** AI reads each payment, so automations need Plus. They're included in your free trial. See [plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/).
- **The iPhone app, signed in.** Open MoneyMatter once after installing it, so Shortcuts can find its actions.
- **iOS version.** Wallet and Message automations work on iOS 17 and later. Notification automations need iOS 27.

## Set up a Wallet automation

Wallet only reports payments you make by tapping your phone or watch in a shop. Online and in-app Apple Pay payments don't trigger it.

<PhoneVideo
  src="/videos/iphone/wallet-setup.mp4"
  label="Screen recording of setting up a Wallet automation with the Log Wallet Payment action, from an empty Automation list to the saved automation."
/>

<Steps>

1. Open **Shortcuts**, go to **Library** → **Automation** and tap **+**. If a card asks you to describe the shortcut, tap **Edit**.

2. In the action list, tap the **Automation** chip and choose **Wallet** under **Apps**. The trigger reads **When Any Card is tapped**.

   <TriggerCard summary="When {Any Card} is tapped" />

3. Choose which card the automation listens to:

   - Tap **Any Card** and pick one card. Do this once per card, so each automation can send its payments to the right account. **Recommended.**
   - Or keep **Any Card**. Every tap gets logged, including cards you don't track in MoneyMatter.

4. Tap the arrow next to the trigger to see its options, and check that the **Automation** toggle is on.

5. In the search field at the bottom, search for **Log Wallet** and add **Log Wallet Payment**.

   <ActionCard
     title="Log Wallet Payment"
     fields={[
       { label: 'Amount' },
       { label: 'Merchant' },
       { label: 'Card' },
       { label: 'Location', value: 'Choose' },
       { label: 'Account' },
     ]}
   />

6. Fill **Amount** with the payment amount:

   1. Tap the **Amount** field. A row of choices opens: **Select Variable**, **Ask Each Time** and more. Swipe it all the way to the right and tap **Transaction**. Or tap **Select Variable**, then tap the **Transaction** bubble under the trigger.

      <VariableBar target="Transaction" />

   2. A blue <VariablePill label="Transaction" /> pill fills the field. Tap the pill and pick **Amount** from the **Get** list. The pill now reads <VariablePill label="Amount" />.

      <Screenshot
        phone
        src="/screenshots/iphone/wallet-variable-get.png"
        alt="The Shortcuts editor with a Transaction pill in the Amount field of Log Wallet Payment, and the Get list open below it: Transaction, Card or Pass, Merchant, Amount and Name."
        hint="iPhone screenshot, light and dark. In the Wallet automation, insert the Transaction variable into Amount, then tap the pill so the Get list opens."
      />

7. Fill the other two fields the same way: **Merchant** → **Merchant**, **Card** → **Card or Pass**.

8. Optional: tap **Account** and pick the MoneyMatter account this card belongs to. Every payment from this automation then lands on that account.

   <ActionCard
     title="Log Wallet Payment"
     fields={[
       { label: 'Amount', pill: 'Amount' },
       { label: 'Merchant', pill: 'Merchant' },
       { label: 'Card', pill: 'Card or Pass' },
       { label: 'Location', value: 'Choose' },
       { label: 'Account', value: 'Everyday Card · USD' },
     ]}
     caption="A finished action, with an account picked."
   />

9. Tap the back arrow to save the automation.

</Steps>

<Aside title="On iOS 26 and earlier">
Open the **Automation** tab and tap **+**. The trigger is called **Transaction** on iOS 17 and 18, and **Wallet** on iOS 26. Pick your card, choose **Run Immediately** so iOS doesn't ask you to confirm every time, tap **Next**, then **New Blank Automation**. Continue from step 5.
</Aside>

### Add where you paid

The **Location** field saves the shop's location with the transaction.

<Steps>

1. In the automation, add the **Get Current Location** action above **Log Wallet Payment**.
2. Tap **Location** and pick the **Current Location** variable.

## Set up an SMS automation

Use this if your bank texts you about every payment.

<Steps>

1. Start a new automation as for Wallet, but choose **Message**.
2. Set **Sender** to your bank, and add **Message Contains** with a phrase that only its payment texts use (for example, the word your bank puts before the amount). Keep the filter narrow so one-time codes and promotions aren't captured.
3. Add the MoneyMatter action **Log Payment Message**.
4. Tap **Message Text**, insert the **Message** variable, then tap the pill and pick **Content**.

   <VariableBar target="Message" />

5. Optional: fill **Sender** the same way with **Message** → **Sender**.
6. Optional: pick an **Account**.

   <ActionCard
     title="Log Payment Message"
     fields={[
       { label: 'Message Text', pill: 'Content' },
       { label: 'Sender', pill: 'Sender' },
       { label: 'Account' },
     ]}
   />

7. Tap the back arrow to save.

</Steps>

## Set up a notification automation

Use this if your bank's app sends a notification for every payment. Needs iOS 27.

<Steps>

1. Start a new automation as for Wallet, but choose **Notification** under **Apps**. The trigger reads **When I receive a notification from App**.

   <TriggerCard summary="When I receive a notification from {App}" />

2. Tap **App** and pick your bank's app. Then tap **Add Filter**, for example **Title contains** or **Message contains**, with words only payment notifications use. That way promotions and sign-in alerts don't run the automation.
3. Add the MoneyMatter action **Log Payment Message**.
4. Tap **Message Text**, insert the **Notification** variable, then tap the pill and pick **Body**. This is the only field you have to fill.

   <PropertyList options={['Body', 'App', 'Sender']} pick="Body" />

5. Optional: fill **Sender** with **Notification** → **App**. Don't pick the notification's **Sender** property: it means the person who sent a message, so it's empty for bank notifications.
6. Optional: pick an **Account**.

   <ActionCard
     title="Log Payment Message"
     fields={[
       { label: 'Message Text', pill: 'Body' },
       { label: 'Sender', pill: 'App' },
       { label: 'Account' },
     ]}
   />

7. Tap the back arrow to save.

</Steps>

## Where the payments go

- **They're imported the next time you open the app.** The automation only queues the payment on your phone. Open MoneyMatter and it's sent off and added.
- **Bank-connected accounts** get a planned transaction. It's confirmed when your bank sync reports the same amount. If the bank never reports it, delete the planned transaction.
- **Manual accounts** get a regular transaction.
- **No account picked in the shortcut?** The first payment from each card asks which account it belongs to. Tap the sync icon on the **Home** screen: it's listed under **SMS & Wallet** → **Check these payments** as "Pick the account for this card".
- **Different currency?** A payment in a currency other than its account's waits in the same list, with the original amount filled in.

## Privacy

- The message text and sender, and the Wallet amount, merchant and card, are sent to the MoneyMatter server and its AI provider to be read, then removed from your phone.
- The location is saved with the transaction on the MoneyMatter server only. It's never sent to the AI provider.

## Upgrading from Log Transaction

Earlier versions of the app had a single **Log Transaction** action. It's now two actions: **Log Wallet Payment** and **Log Payment Message**.

- **SMS and notification automations keep working.** The action now shows as **Log Payment Message**. Nothing to change.
- **Wallet automations need rebuilding.** Open each one, delete the old action and add **Log Wallet Payment** as in [Set up a Wallet automation](#set-up-a-wallet-automation).

<Aside type="tip">
While you're there, pick an **Account** in each automation. Payments then skip the "which account?" question.
</Aside>

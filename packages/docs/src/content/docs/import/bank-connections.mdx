A bank connection brings your transactions and balances into MoneyMatter for you, so you don't have to type them in or import files. You connect once, pick the accounts you want, and MoneyMatter keeps them up to date.

## Who can use bank connections <Badge text="Plus" variant="tip" />

Bank connections are part of the Plus plan, and they're included in your free trial. On the Essential plan, clicking **Connect bank** shows **Included in the Plus plan** with a **See plans** button. See [plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/).

## Supported banks and services

MoneyMatter connects through five providers. Some talk to one bank directly, others connect you to thousands of banks.

| Provider | Where | Cost | Setup | What you need |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lunch Flow** | USA, EU, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand. 20,000+ banks. | **Paid** | **Easy to setup** | A Lunch Flow account with your banks connected, and its **API Key** (in Lunch Flow: Settings → API Destinations). |
| **SimpleFIN** | USA and Canada | **Paid** | **Easy to setup** | A SimpleFIN Bridge account with your bank linked, and a **Setup token** from the Bridge dashboard. |
| **Monobank** | Ukraine | **Free** | **Easy to setup** | A personal **API Token**, which you create at api.monobank.ua. The token is read-only. |
| **Enable Banking** (**Beta**) | EU and UK. 6,000+ banks through open banking. | **Free** | **Very difficult to setup** | Your own Enable Banking developer application: its **Application ID** and **Private Key**. |
| **Walutomat** | Poland. The currency exchange platform, with 23 currencies. | **Free** | **Medium setup** | An **API Key** and **Private Key (PEM)**. You create a key pair and upload the public key in Walutomat (Settings → API). |

"Paid" means the provider charges for its own service, separately from your MoneyMatter plan. Lunch Flow and SimpleFIN are independent services that connect to your bank for you.

<Aside type="caution" title="Enable Banking is for technical users">
Enable Banking doesn't offer an affordable ready-made connection, so you register your own developer application with them and give MoneyMatter its credentials. Expect some technical setup. The connection form links to step-by-step instructions.
</Aside>

## Connect a bank

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Accounts** → **All Accounts**.
2. In the **Bank connections** section, click **Connect bank**.
3. In **Add Integration**, pick your region to narrow the list (**All**, **US & Canada**, **EU & UK**, **Ukraine** or **Poland**), then choose a provider. If you chose Enable Banking, follow [Connect with Enable Banking](#connect-with-enable-banking) instead.
4. Paste your credentials and click **Connect**. Each form has a help link, such as **Where do I get an API token?**, with instructions for getting them.
5. Choose the accounts to add, then click the button below the list (see [Choosing accounts](#choosing-accounts)).

</Steps>

You'll see "Transactions are syncing in the background". The accounts appear right away, and their transactions follow over the next few minutes.

### Choosing accounts

What you see after **Connect** depends on the provider:

- **Monobank**: tick the accounts you want to sync, then click **Sync 2 account(s)** (the number matches your selection).
- **SimpleFIN**: tick the accounts, then click **Import 2 accounts**.
- **Walutomat**: each currency wallet is its own account. Tick the wallets, then click **Import 2 wallet(s)**.
- **Lunch Flow**: all accounts from your Lunch Flow account are imported together. Click **Import 3 account(s)**, or **Skip** if you only want some of them. You can then add specific accounts later from the connection's page, or link them to accounts you already have.

If the bank doesn't say which currency an account uses, you'll be asked to **Choose currency**. Choose carefully: it can't be changed later.

### Connect with Enable Banking

Enable Banking sends you to your bank's website to approve access:

<Steps>

1. Choose **Enable Banking**, enter your **Application ID** and **Private Key**, and click **Next**.
2. Select your bank's country, then your bank.
3. Click **Authorize with** and your bank's name. You're taken to your bank's website.
4. Log in at your bank and approve the connection.
5. Back in MoneyMatter, you'll see **Connection Successful!** and then the connection's page.
6. Click **Connect Accounts**, tick the accounts you want, and click **Connect Selected Accounts**.

</Steps>

Finish in the same browser you started in. MoneyMatter remembers the pending connection in that browser, and elsewhere you'll see "Connection ID not found. Please start the connection process again."

## What happens to your connected accounts

**Connecting always creates new accounts in MoneyMatter.** Each one:

- takes its name from your bank,
- starts with the balance your bank reports,
- goes into an [account group](/accounts/account-groups/) named after the connection.

The only exception: if an account was connected to the same bank account before and you disconnected it, connecting again reuses that account instead of making a new one.

You can't connect the same bank account twice through two connections to the same provider. You'll see "is already connected through" and the other connection's name. Disconnect it there first.

Connected accounts appear in the **Bank connections** section of the **Accounts** page. Each connection shows its status (**Active**, **Expiring soon**, **Expired** or **Reconnect**), how many accounts it has and their total. Click it to see its accounts, or click the gear button (**Manage connection**) to open its page.

## Link an account you already have

Already tracking an account by hand? Instead of creating a new one, you can connect it to your bank and keep its history.

<Steps>

1. Connect the bank first (see [connect a bank](#connect-a-bank)), and don't select the account you're about to link. With Lunch Flow, click **Skip** to connect without importing any accounts.
2. Open the account you track by hand and select the **Data Connection** tab.
3. Under **Link account to bank connection**, click **Link to bank**.
4. Choose the **Bank Connection**, then the **External Account** at your bank.
5. Check the balances shown, and click **Link Account**.

</Steps>

Both accounts must use the same currency. If they don't, you'll see **⚠️ Cannot Link Account**.

What linking does:

- **Your existing transactions stay as they are.**
- **The history you entered yourself isn't downloaded again.** With Monobank, SimpleFIN and Lunch Flow, syncing starts from the moment you link. With Enable Banking and Walutomat, it starts from your newest transaction in the account.
- **The balance becomes your bank's balance.** Any difference between the two is absorbed into the account's initial balance. No extra transaction is created.
- **The account now follows the bank's rules.** See [what you can edit](#what-you-can-edit).

## How syncing works

### Automatic sync

When you open MoneyMatter, it checks when your banks last synced. If it's been 12 hours or more, it syncs all your connected accounts in the background.

There's no sync while you're away: if you don't open the app for a week, your accounts catch up the next time you do.

### Sync now

To sync right away, use any of these:

- **The cloud button in the header.** It opens **Banks connections sync status**, which shows when your accounts last synced and, while a sync runs, each account's status (**Syncing**, **Queued**, **Synced**, **Failed** or **Idle**). Click **Sync Now**, or **Retry sync** after a failed sync.
- **The account's page.** On the **Data Connection** tab, next to **Sync latest transactions**, click **Sync**.
- **The connection's page.** Click **Sync now** in the **Connected Accounts** section.

If you use **Sync Now** in the header less than 4 hours after the last sync, MoneyMatter asks first: **Sync Recently Completed**. Click **Sync Anyway** to go ahead.

### How far back the first sync goes

| Provider | History fetched when you connect |
|---|---|
| Monobank | The last 31 days |
| SimpleFIN | The last 6 months |
| Walutomat | The last 12 months |
| Enable Banking | As much as your bank allows: about 3 years, 2 years, 1 year or 90 days, depending on the bank |
| Lunch Flow | Whatever Lunch Flow has for the account |

After that, each sync picks up from your newest transaction.

### Load older history

For **Monobank** and **SimpleFIN** accounts, you can fetch older transactions yourself:

<Steps>

1. Open the account and select the **Data Connection** tab.
2. Next to **Load transactions for selected period**, click the period button. It shows **Current month** until you pick another range.
3. Choose a date range of up to one year. For more, repeat with another range.

</Steps>

Monobank only allows one request a minute, so a long period loads in the background in monthly chunks and can take a while.

For an account you [linked](#link-an-account-you-already-have), you can't load transactions from before the day you linked it.

### What synced transactions look like

- **The balance comes from your bank.** After each sync, the account's balance is set to what your bank reports.
- **New transactions start in your default category** (usually **Other**), unless the payee has a [default category](/transactions/payees/#default-category). MoneyMatter then tries to categorize them automatically with AI. Review the categories and fix any it got wrong.
- **Transfers between your own connected accounts are linked automatically.** When MoneyMatter finds exactly one match with the same amount and currency, in the opposite direction, within 3 days, it turns the pair into a [transfer](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/). To also match against accounts you track by hand, turn on [Match transfers with manual accounts](/settings/general-settings/#match-transfers-with-manual-accounts).
- **Walutomat currency exchanges** between your wallets are linked as transfers.
- **Pending card payments**: with Enable Banking, they're saved only if you turn on [Import pending bank transactions](/settings/general-settings/#import-pending-bank-transactions), and when the bank books the payment, the pending copy is merged into it. Monobank saves card payments that are still on hold straight away, and those aren't linked as transfers automatically. You can [link them yourself](/transfers-and-refunds/link-existing-transactions/).

## What you can edit

Transactions in a bank-connected account belong to your bank, so some details are locked to keep MoneyMatter matching your bank:

| You can change | You can't change |
|---|---|
| Category, note, payee and tags | Amount, date and time, type (income or expense), payment type, account |

You also can't:

- **Delete** these transactions. The **Delete** button doesn't appear. To get rid of a duplicate or a wrong one, use [Reconciliation](/transactions/reconciliation/), where you can merge or remove it and undo that later.
- **Add** transactions to a bank-connected account by hand. They arrive from your bank.

<Aside type="caution" title="Don't import files into a connected account">
The CSV, OFX, Wallet and Microsoft Money importers still let you pick a bank-connected account. Avoid it: the imported rows can duplicate transactions your bank syncs, and the import can't be undone from [Import History](/import/import-from-csv/#undo-an-import).
</Aside>

You can still make a synced transaction part of a transfer. See [transfers with bank-connected accounts](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#transfers-with-bank-connected-accounts-).

## Why you won't see duplicates

- **Each bank transaction is saved once.** MoneyMatter remembers the bank's own ID for every transaction and skips ones it already has, even after you disconnect and reconnect the account. The exception is Monobank: after reconnecting, the most recent transaction from before you disconnected can come in a second time.
- **Manual and synced transactions don't overlap.** You can't add transactions to a connected account by hand, and a [linked account](#link-an-account-you-already-have) doesn't download the history you already entered. File imports are the exception, see [what you can edit](#what-you-can-edit).
- **Pending payments merge with the booked ones** (Enable Banking only), so a card payment doesn't appear twice when the bank books it a few days later.

If a duplicate slips through anyway, or a pending payment stays pending for more than a week, fix it in [Reconciliation](/transactions/reconciliation/).

## Manage a connection

Open the connection's page from the gear button (**Manage connection**) on the **Accounts** page. It shows:

- the connection's name, whether it's **Active**, and when it was connected,
- the **Total balance**, how many **Accounts** are connected and the **Last sync**,
- for Enable Banking, how many days of **Consent** are left,
- the **Connected Accounts** list.

The **Manage** menu at the top has **Rename connection** and **Disconnect Integration**.

If your bank has accounts you didn't add at first, click **Connect remaining accounts** (or **Connect Accounts** if none are connected yet). In **Connect Remaining Accounts**, tick the ones you want and click **Connect Selected Accounts**. Accounts that are already in MoneyMatter are marked **Already connected**.

### Rename a connection

Click **Manage** → **Rename connection**, enter a new **Connection Name** and click **Save**.

## Reconnect a connection

### When Enable Banking consent runs out

Banks only let an app like MoneyMatter read your data for a limited time. MoneyMatter asks for the longest period your bank allows, or 90 days if the bank doesn't say. When that time is almost up (7 days or fewer), the connection shows **Expiring soon**. After that it shows **Expired** and stops syncing. You'll also see **Connections need reconnecting** in the header's sync popover, and **Connection expired** on the account's **Data Connection** tab.

To renew it:

<Steps>

1. Open the connection's page.
2. In the strip that says when consent expires, click **Reconnect Now**.
3. Read **Reconnect Integration** and click **Continue**. Your current connection stops working immediately, so finish the next steps.
4. Log in at your bank and approve access again.

</Steps>

Your accounts are matched up again and keep syncing into the same MoneyMatter accounts, with their history intact.

### When your credentials stop working

For the other providers, a connection is switched off when the bank or service keeps rejecting your credentials. The connection's page then says "This connection needs to be reconnected before it can sync again". Click **Update Credentials**. The field is labelled **API Key** for every provider: paste your new Monobank API token, a new SimpleFIN Setup token or your new Lunch Flow API key. For Walutomat, also paste the **Private Key (PEM)**. Then click **Update**.

## Disconnect a bank

<Steps>

1. Open the connection's page.
2. Click **Manage** → **Disconnect Integration**.
3. Decide whether to tick **Also remove associated accounts**.
4. Click **Disconnect**.

</Steps>

What happens depends on the checkbox:

- **Unticked (the default):** your accounts and all their transactions stay. They become regular accounts that you update yourself, and syncing stops. Their transactions become regular transactions too, so you can edit and delete them freely.
- **Ticked:** the connected accounts and all their transactions are permanently deleted.

Disconnecting can't be undone. If you kept the accounts and connect the same bank again later, MoneyMatter reuses them and recognises transactions it already has, so nothing is duplicated. With Monobank, the most recent transaction from before you disconnected can be synced a second time, so check the newest entries.

### Unlink just one account

To stop syncing one account but keep the rest of the connection:

<Steps>

1. Open the account and select the **Data Connection** tab.
2. In the **Danger zone**, click **Unlink from bank**.
3. Type the account's name to confirm, and confirm.

</Steps>

The account keeps all its transactions and becomes an account you update yourself. You can link it again later from the same tab.

## If you leave the Plus plan

Your connected accounts and their transactions stay, and you can still see your connections. But you can't add, sync, reconnect, rename or disconnect them until you're back on Plus. See [plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/#when-a-feature-isnt-in-your-plan).

You can still unlink individual accounts: open the account, select **Data Connection** and click **Unlink from bank**. The account and its transactions stay, and you can edit them normally.

## Good to know

- **Automatic sync only runs when you open the app**, at most every 12 hours. Click **Sync Now** whenever you want fresh data.
- **Pick the currency carefully** when the bank doesn't report one. It can't be changed afterwards.
- **Imports into a connected account can't be undone.** Once an account is connected to a bank, earlier [imports](/import/import-from-csv/#undo-an-import) into it can't be deleted from Import History.
- **The demo can't connect banks.** See [Try the demo](/getting-started/quick-start/#try-the-demo).

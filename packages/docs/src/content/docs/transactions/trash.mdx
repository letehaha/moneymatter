When you delete a transaction, it isn't gone straight away. It moves to **Trash**, where it stays for 30 days. Until then you can bring it back with everything that was attached to it.

## What goes to Trash

Every transaction you delete goes to Trash, whether you delete it from its own window (see [delete a transaction](/transactions/add-and-edit/#delete-a-transaction)) or [several at once](/transactions/search-and-filter/#delete-several-transactions).

- **A transfer goes as a pair.** Deleting either side moves both sides to Trash, and they come back together. See [delete a transfer](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#delete-a-transfer).
- **A loan payment goes as a pair too**: the expense on your account and the income on the loan.
- **A trashed transaction stops counting.** Account balances are recalculated without it, and it leaves your stats and transaction lists.

Transactions removed in [reconciliation](/transactions/reconciliation/) don't go to Trash. You restore those from the reconciliation **History** tab. See [restore from History](/transactions/reconciliation/#restore-from-history).

## Undo a delete

Right after you delete, a **Moved to Trash** message appears with an **Undo** button. Click it within about 8 seconds to put the transaction back where it was. A bulk delete shows how many moved, for example **3 transactions moved to Trash**, and **Undo** brings all of them back.

If you miss it, restore the transaction from Trash instead.

## Open Trash

Open **All transactions** in the sidebar and click **Trash** at the top of the page. On a phone, **Trash** is in the **More** menu (⋯) instead.

Trash lists your deleted transactions, newest deletions first, grouped by how many days each has left, such as **12 days left**. When some will be deleted within the next 7 days, a banner at the top tells you how many.

Click a transaction to see its details. Next to the list, or in a window on a narrow screen, **Comes back with** lists what returns with it: its category, note and payee, its splits, tags and attachments, the [tracker](/planned/trackers/), [group](/transactions/groups/), [recurring payment](/planned/recurring-payments/) or refund it was linked to, and, for a transfer, the other side.

## Restore a transaction

<Steps>

1. Open **Trash**.
2. Click the transaction.
3. Click **Restore**.

</Steps>

The transaction goes back to its account, and the balance is recalculated. Its splits, tags, attachments and links come back with it. A link to a transaction that's also in Trash stays hidden until you restore that one too.

A refund link that no longer fits, for example because the other transaction changed in the meantime, is dropped, and the message tells you how many.

Some transactions can't come back:

- **The account was connected to a bank since.** A manual transaction dated on or after the day you linked the account to the bank can't be restored, because from that day the bank provides the transactions. See [accounts you linked yourself](/import/bank-connections/#accounts-you-linked-yourself).
- **The other side of a loan payment is gone.** A loan payment can only come back with both sides.

## Delete a transaction for good

Trash empties itself: 30 days after you deleted a transaction, it's removed for good.

To remove one right away:

<Steps>

1. Open **Trash** and click the transaction.
2. Click **Delete permanently** (just **Delete** when the panel is narrow).
3. Confirm in the **Delete permanently?** window.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
A transaction deleted permanently can't be restored.
</Aside>

[Undoing an import](/import/import-from-csv/#undo-an-import) also deletes for good any of that import's transactions that are in Trash.

## What can't be moved to Trash

Some transactions have to be unlinked first. The delete is refused with a message that tells you what to do:

- **Transactions linked to a portfolio.** [Unlink it from the portfolio](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#to-a-portfolio) first, or delete it from the portfolio's **Activity** tab.
- **Transactions linked to a venture deal.** Delete the event on the deal instead. See [what linking does to a transaction](/investments/venture/#what-linking-does-to-a-transaction).
- **Part of a payment from several accounts.** Remove it from the payment first.

Some can't be deleted at all, so they never reach Trash:

- **Transactions from a bank connection**, and manual ones dated on or after the day you linked the account to a bank. See [bank-synced transactions](/transactions/add-and-edit/#bank-synced-transactions).
- **The other person's side** of a transfer into an account someone shares with you. See [household and sharing](/settings/household-and-sharing/).

## Good to know

- **Portfolios have their own Trash**, at the bottom of the **Investments** page. See [delete and restore a portfolio](/investments/portfolios/#delete-and-restore-a-portfolio).

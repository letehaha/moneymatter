The **All Transactions** page shows every transaction across your accounts. Filters narrow it down, and selecting rows lets you change or delete many transactions in one go.

To open it, go to the sidebar and choose **Transactions** → **All Transactions**.

## List view and table view

Use the toggle at the top of the page to switch between **List view** and **Table view**.

- **List view** is a compact list. On a wide screen, the filters sit in a panel on the left.
- **Table view** shows your transactions as columns you can sort, with the filters in a toolbar across the top.

On a computer the page opens in **Table view**; on a phone it opens in **List view**. Your choice is remembered separately for each, so you can keep the table on your laptop and the list on your phone. "Phone" here means a narrow page, so a small browser window counts too.

### Working with the table

- **Open a transaction:** click its row.
- **Sort:** click a column header. You can sort by **Date**, **Category**, **Amount**, **Base amount**, **Account**, **Payee**, **Note** and **Categorized by**. The default is **Date**, newest first.
- **Choose columns:** open **Table settings** to show, hide and reorder columns, or click **Reset to defaults**. The available columns are **Date**, **Account**, **Category**, **Payee**, **Amount**, **Base amount**, **Original amount**, **Note**, **Tags**, **Categorized by**, **Group**, **Refund** and **Split**. **Categorized by** shows what set each transaction's category, for example **Manual** when you changed the category on an existing transaction (a category picked while creating the transaction leaves this blank), or **Payee rule** when a payee set to **Always use payee category** filled it in.
- **Focus mode:** click **Focus mode** to hide the rest of the app and give the table the whole screen. Press Esc or click **Close** (**Exit focus mode** on a phone) to return.

Sorting by **Amount** uses each transaction's value in your base currency, so a list with several currencies still sorts in a sensible order.

<Aside type="note" title="Transfers show as one row">
A transfer between two of your accounts is really two linked transactions, but the list shows it once, on the side the money left. See [transfers in the transaction list](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#transfers-in-the-transaction-list).
</Aside>

## Filter transactions

Filters apply by themselves a moment after you change them; there's no Apply button. Click **Reset** to clear them all.

Where the filters are depends on the view:

- **Table view:** the date, accounts and categories filters are always in the toolbar. Add others from the **Filters** menu. Each extra filter has a **Remove filter** button.
- **List view on a wide screen:** all filters are in the left panel.
- **Narrow screens:** tap **Filters** to open them.

Your filters and sorting stay in place while you move around the app in the same browser tab. Closing the tab clears them.

### Available filters

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Date | Pick a date or a range, or use a preset: **This month**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 12 months**. |
| Accounts | Only transactions on the accounts you pick. Archived accounts are listed too. |
| Categories | Only transactions in the categories you pick. Picking a parent category also picks all its subcategories. |
| **Transaction type** | **All types**, **Income** or **Expense**. |
| **Amount range** | A minimum and/or maximum amount. |
| **Tags** | Transactions with any of the tags you pick. Choose **No tags** to find untagged transactions. |
| **Payees** | Transactions with any of the payees you pick. Choose **No payee** to find ones without a payee. |
| **Refunds** | **All**, **Exclude** or **Only** transactions that are part of a refund link: both the refund and the purchase it refunds. |
| **Transfers** | **All**, **Exclude** or **Only** transfers. |
| **Transfer kinds** | Narrow transfers down by kind, such as **Between accounts** or **Out of wallet** (see [how transfers work](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/)). Has no effect when **Transfers** is set to **Exclude**. |
| **Attachments** | **All**, **Exclude** or **Only** transactions that have attachments. |

A few details worth knowing:

- **The amount filter uses each transaction's own currency**, not your base currency. A filter of "at least 100" matches 100 EUR on a euro account and 100 USD on a dollar account.
- **The categories filter also looks inside splits.** A transaction split between Groceries and Drug-store, chemist shows up when you filter by either one, and the whole transaction is listed. See [split a transaction](/transactions/split-a-transaction/).
- **Refunds and Transfers both set to Only** show transactions that are refunds *or* transfers.

### Search

The search box at the top of the list looks through transaction notes, payee names and tag names. Results update as you type.

It ignores upper and lower case, and matches any part of the text, so "amaz" finds "Amazon order".

Type several words to narrow the results. A transaction matches only if it contains **every** word, each in its note, payee or tags. For example, `starbucks latte` finds a Starbucks transaction with "latte" in its note.

<Aside type="caution" title="Categories and amounts aren't searched">
To find transactions by category or amount, use the **Categories** or **Amount** filter.
</Aside>

### Open the list from a category

In **Settings** → **Categories**, the menu next to a category has **View transactions**. It opens this page filtered to that one category. Unlike picking a parent in the categories filter, this doesn't include its subcategories. See [categories](/transactions/categories/).

## Select several transactions

Tick the checkbox on a row to select it. You can also:

- Hold **Shift** and click another checkbox to select everything in between.
- Tick the checkbox in the table's header row (in list view, **Select all**) to select every transaction currently loaded.

The bar at the top shows how many rows are selected, with totals in your base currency: **Income**, **Expense**, **Net** and **Transfers**. Transfers between your own accounts are shown on their own and never counted in **Net**, since moving money between your accounts doesn't make you richer or poorer. Out of Wallet transfers, including balance adjustments, count as income or expense here, even though your [stats](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/) leave them out.

<Aside type="note" title="Good to know">
- **Selecting all only picks what's loaded.** The page loads more transactions as you scroll, so scroll to the end first if you want everything that matches.
- **Changing filters or sorting clears the selection.** The list reloads from the top, so you start a fresh selection.
- **Split transactions can't be selected.** Bulk editing doesn't support them, so edit them one at a time.
</Aside>

With rows selected you can **Edit**, **Group** or **Delete** them. On a phone, list view puts these actions in a menu; table view shows them as icon buttons. In table view, **Cancel selection** clears the selection.

### Edit several transactions at once

<Steps>

1. Select the transactions.
2. Click **Edit**. The **Edit N transactions** window opens.
3. Fill in only what you want to change: **Category**, **Payee**, **Tags** or **Note**. Empty fields are left alone.
4. If you picked tags, choose what happens to them with the tabs above the **Tags** field:
   - **Add** (the default) keeps the tags each transaction already has.
   - **Replace** swaps them for the ones you picked.
   - **Remove** takes the picked tags off.
5. Click **Apply changes**, check the summary in **Confirm changes**, and click **Apply**.

</Steps>

Picking a payee also fills in that payee's auto-apply tags, which you can remove before applying.

Things to watch for:

- **A new note replaces the old one** on every selected transaction. It isn't added to the end. If you also change the category, transfers in the selection keep their old note. Edit notes on transfers in a separate bulk edit.
- **You can't clear a field in bulk.** Leaving the note or payee empty means "don't change", not "remove".
- **Category changes skip transfers.** Transfers don't use categories, so they keep what they have. If you selected only transfers and set a category, the edit is refused.
- **Payees set in bulk are locked**, the same as when you set them by hand. Automatic payee detection won't change them later.

### Group transactions

A group bundles related transactions under one name, for example a salary and the transfer you made from it, or all the costs of a trip.

<Steps>

1. Select at least two transactions.
2. Click **Group** → **Create new group**.
3. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Note (optional)**, then click **Create Group**.

</Steps>

To add transactions to a group you already have:

<Steps>

1. Select one or more transactions.
2. Click **Group** → **Add to existing group**.
3. Pick the group. Use **Search groups...** if you have many.
4. Click **Add to Group**.

</Steps>

- A group can hold 2 to 50 transactions, and a transaction can only be in one group.
- Groups are just labels. They don't change any amounts or stats.
- In list view, a group shows as one row. It expands into its transactions when any filter other than the date is active.
- Deleting a group keeps its transactions.

You'll find your groups in the sidebar under **Transactions** → **Groups**. To see a group's total and learn when a group beats a tag or category, see [Transaction groups](/transactions/groups/).

### Delete several transactions

<Steps>

1. Select the transactions.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm in the **Delete N transactions?** window.

</Steps>

This can't be undone. If a selected transaction is one side of a transfer, both sides are deleted.

If any selected transaction came from a bank connection, the **Delete** button is disabled and nothing is deleted. Bank-synced transactions can't be deleted, so remove them from the selection first. If a selected transfer has its other side on a bank-connected account, the bulk delete fails and nothing in the selection is deleted. Take that transfer out of the selection. Transfers like this can't be deleted, not even one at a time. See [bank-synced transactions](/transactions/add-and-edit/#bank-synced-transactions).

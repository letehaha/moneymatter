A transaction is a single movement of money: a coffee you paid for, a salary that came in, money you moved between accounts. This page shows how to add one by hand, what each field in the form does, and what you can change later.

## Add a transaction

<Steps>

1. Click **Add transaction** in the header at the top of the app. On a phone the button is called **Add**.
2. Keep **Expense**, or switch to **Income**. New transactions start as **Expense**. (To move money between your own accounts, pick **Transfer** instead. See [how transfers work](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/).)
3. Enter the **Amount**.
4. Check the **Account**. It's usually filled in for you (see [Account](#account)).
5. Check the **Category**. It starts with the first category in your list, so pick the right one.
6. Check the **Datetime**. It starts at the current date and time.
7. Optionally pick a **Payee** and fill in anything under **More options**, such as a **Note** or **Tags**.
8. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

The transaction appears in your list right away, and the account balance updates.

To record money moving between two of your own accounts, pick **Transfer** instead. It has **From account** and **To account** fields and works differently from income and expenses. See [how transfers work](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/).

### Create several transactions in a row

In the **Add Transaction** form you open from the header, turn on **Create more** next to the **Create** button to keep the form open after each save. You see a **Transaction created** message, and the form clears for the next one. It keeps the type, the account (and the **To account** on a transfer), the **Datetime** and the **Payment type**; everything else starts fresh. **Planned** goes back to the account's default, and on an account that isn't bank-connected a future **Datetime** resets to now. **Create more** switches off again when you close the form. To keep the form open for just one save, press **Ctrl + Shift + Enter** (**⌘ + Shift + Enter** on a Mac).

## The fields

The main column holds the fields you need every time. Everything else lives in the **More options** panel: a column on the right on a computer, or a **More options** button at the bottom of the form on a phone. On a phone, the button shows how many of those fields are filled in.

### Expense, Income or Transfer

The tab decides the direction of the money. An expense lowers the account balance, and income raises it. The amount itself is always a positive number, so you never type a minus sign.

You can switch tabs while editing too, as long as the transaction is on a manual account. Bank-synced transactions have [some limits](#bank-synced-transactions).

### Amount

Enter the amount in the account's currency. The currency code shows inside the field. The amount can't be negative, but 0 is allowed.

If the account's currency isn't your base currency, MoneyMatter also works out the amount in your base currency, using the exchange rate for the transaction's date. That converted amount is what your stats use. See [base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/#how-a-transaction-gets-its-base-currency-amount).

### Account

The account the money came from or went to. When you open the form:

- On an account's page, that account is already selected.
- Otherwise, it's your **Default account** from **Settings** → **General**, if you've set one. See [default account](/settings/general-settings/#default-account).
- If you haven't, it's the first account in the list.

Only [manual accounts](/accounts/create-an-account/), the ones you keep up to date yourself, are listed. Accounts connected to a bank aren't offered, because their transactions arrive from the bank itself. Archived accounts are hidden unless you turn on **Show archived accounts** in **Settings** → **General**; then they appear at the end of the list.

### Category

Required for expenses and income. You can pick any category, whether it's a top-level one or a subcategory. The picker has a search box. You can't create a category from here; do that in [categories](/transactions/categories/) first.

The **Split** button next to the field lets you spread one transaction across several categories. See [split a transaction](/transactions/split-a-transaction/).

### Datetime

The date and time of the transaction, in 24-hour format. It starts at the current moment. The calendar only offers dates up to today.

### Payee

Who you paid or who paid you, such as a shop or your employer. Start typing to search your payees. If the one you want doesn't exist, click **Create payee "Lidl"** (it shows whatever you typed) to add it without leaving the form. If you haven't typed anything, the option reads **Create new payee**.

Picking a payee can fill in other fields for you:

- **Category:** the payee's default category, or, if it has none, the category you use most often with it. This only happens if you haven't touched the **Category** field yet.
- **Tags:** the payee's auto-apply tags are added. If you switch to another payee, only those automatic tags are swapped. Tags you added yourself stay.
- **Location:** the payee's default location, if the form doesn't have one yet.

The **Payee** field isn't shown for transfers. Read more in [payees](/transactions/payees/).

<Aside type="caution" title="A payee's default category can replace yours">
If the payee has a default category and its **AI categorization** setting is **Always use payee category** (the default) or **Use as a hint**, that default category is used when the new transaction is saved, even if you picked a different one in the form. To keep your own choice, edit the transaction after saving it (edits don't re-apply the payee's category), or open the payee in **Settings** → **Payees** and set **AI categorization** to **Off** so its default category is no longer forced when you save. See [payees](/transactions/payees/#default-category).
</Aside>

### More options

- **Payment type:** how you paid. Choose from **Credit Card** (the default), **Bank Transfer**, **Cash**, **Debit Card**, **Mobile Payment**, **Voucher** or **Web Payment**.
- **Note:** free text, up to 1,000 characters. You can find transactions by their notes with the [Notes filter](/transactions/search-and-filter/#search-notes).
- **Tags:** labels you can add on top of the category, such as "Need" or "Trip to Rome". You can pick several. Create them in [tags](/transactions/tags/).
- **Attachments:** receipts and invoices. See [attachments](#attachments) below.

### Optional fields

A few extra fields only appear if you turn them on in **Settings** → **General** → **Optional transaction fields**. A field that already has a value on a transaction always shows. By default, only **Original amount** is on. See [optional transaction fields](/settings/general-settings/#optional-transaction-fields).

- **Original amount:** for a purchase made in a currency different from the account's, the amount you actually paid in that currency. Pick the currency next to it. MoneyMatter suggests a value based on the exchange rate for the transaction date, which you can accept with **Apply**. It's for your records only and doesn't affect statistics. Not shown for transfers.
- **External URL:** a link to the order, booking or receipt page. It must start with http:// or https://. The link icon next to the label opens it.
- **External reference:** an order number, invoice number or booking reference, up to 255 characters.
- **Location:** where the purchase happened. Click **Use my location** to fill it from your device, type the latitude and longitude, or click **Pick on map**. The first time you open the map, you're asked to allow it, because the map comes from OpenStreetMap. You can turn the map off later in **Settings** → **General** → **Map picker**.

## Attachments

Keep a photo of a receipt or a PDF invoice with the transaction.

<Steps>

1. In the transaction form, click **Add attachments** under **Attachments**.
2. Drag files onto the **Drop files here or click to browse** area, or click that area to pick files.
3. Close the window when you're done. The field now shows how many files are attached.

</Steps>

Click a file to view it, then use **Open in new tab** or **Download**. You can delete files one by one, or all at once with **Delete all**.

  src="/screenshots/transactions/attachments-dialog.png"

Limits:

- Up to 10 files per transaction.
- Up to 10 MB per file.
- JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF only.
- 1 GB of attachments in total per user.

Large photos are shrunk before upload, so a phone picture takes up much less space than the original.

On a new transaction, the files upload right after you click **Create**. When you're creating a transfer, the **Attachments** field isn't shown; save the transfer first, then open it again to attach files.

Deleting a transaction also deletes its attachments.

<Aside type="note" title="Which plans include attachments">
Attachments are part of the Essential and Plus plans, and you can use them during the free trial. See [plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/).
</Aside>

## Templates

If you enter the same kind of transaction often, like a morning coffee or a monthly rent payment, save it as a template and fill the form in one click. Templates only fill the form; they never create transactions on their own.

Templates are available when you add an expense or income. They aren't available for transfers or when editing.

### Save a template

<Steps>

1. Fill in the form the way you want the template to look.
2. Click **Templates** next to the dialog title (on a phone, it opens from the bottom of the screen).
3. Click **Save current as template**.
4. Give it a **Name**. Names must be unique.
5. Optionally tick **Keep whichever account is selected when I use it** to leave the account out of the template, or **Don't save an amount — I'll type it each time**. An amount only makes sense in one account's currency, so choosing the first option also turns on the second.
6. Click **Save template**.

</Steps>

You can also create one from scratch with **New template**.

### Use a template

<Steps>

1. In **Add Transaction**, click **Templates**.
2. Type in **Find a template…** to filter the list, if you have many.
3. Click the template. The form fills in with its details.
4. Check the fields, add anything missing (such as the amount), and click **Create**.

</Steps>

A template can store the type, account, amount, category, payee, tags, payment type, note and original-amount currency.

### Good to know about templates

- If the form already has splits or a refund link, using a template asks **Apply template?** first, because it replaces them. Click **Use template** to go ahead.
- Deleting a template doesn't change any transaction you created with it.
- If a template's account is archived or can no longer be used, the template shows **account unavailable**. Edit the template to pick another account.
- Deleting a category moves templates to the replacement category you choose, or clears the template's category if the deleted category had no transactions.

## Edit a transaction

<Steps>

1. Click the transaction in any list, for example on the **All Transactions** page or an account's page.
2. Change what you need in the **Edit Transaction** form.
3. Click **Edit** to save.

</Steps>

On a manual account you can change every field, including moving the transaction to another manual account or switching between **Expense**, **Income** and **Transfer**.

A few changes have side effects:

- **Changing the payee by hand locks it.** Whether you set, change or clear the payee, automatic payee detection will never change it on that transaction again.
- **Switching a split transaction to Transfer removes its splits** when you save. There's no warning, so check first.
- **Editing doesn't re-apply the payee's default category.** Your choice stays.

To change many transactions at once, use [bulk edit](/transactions/search-and-filter/#edit-several-transactions-at-once).

### Duplicate a transaction

To add a transaction like one you already have, open it and click **Duplicate**. The **Add Transaction** form opens with everything you see in the form copied over, including changes you haven't saved, and dated now. The original stays as it was. Refund links and attachments aren't copied. A copy on a bank-connected account starts as planned, since only the bank adds real transactions there. That's also why **Duplicate** isn't offered on a bank-connected account shared with you, or for a planned transaction on any account shared with you: only the owner can add plans. It isn't offered on loan or vehicle accounts either.

You can also duplicate a transfer between two of your own accounts, including one between accounts in different currencies, and an [out of wallet](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#out-of-wallet-transfers) transfer. The copy opens on the **Transfer** tab going the same way as the original, from the same **From account** to the same **To account**, whichever side you opened. It keeps both amounts, the note, the tags and the payment type, but not the external link, reference or location. On a transfer between currencies, check that the received amount still matches today's rate. **Duplicate** isn't offered for:

- transfers to or from an investment portfolio, a venture or a vehicle
- loan payments
- transfers where either account is bank-connected
- transfers involving an account shared with you that you can't add transactions to

## Bank-synced transactions

Transactions that arrive through a [bank connection](/import/bank-connections/) mirror what your bank reports. Some fields are locked so your records don't drift away from the bank's.

| You can't change | You can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount** | **Category** and splits |
| **Datetime** | **Payee** |
| **Account** | **Note** and **Tags** |
| **Payment type** | **Attachments** |
| Expense ↔ Income | Optional fields, such as **Location** |
| | Refund links, and turning it into a transfer |

On a bank-synced expense, the **Income** tab is disabled, and the other way round. You can still turn the transaction into a transfer. See [turn an expense or income into a transfer](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#turn-an-expense-or-income-into-a-transfer).

Bank-synced transactions **can't be deleted**, so the **Delete** button isn't shown. The bank sync owns them, and deleting one would put your balance out of step with the bank. The same goes for a transfer when either side is on a bank-connected account.

## Delete a transaction

<Steps>

1. Open the transaction.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm with **Delete** in the **Delete this transaction?** window.

</Steps>

This can't be undone. What else happens:

- Its splits and attachments are deleted too.
- Any refund links are removed. The transactions on the other side of those links stay.
- **Deleting one side of a transfer deletes both sides**, since a transfer only makes sense as a pair. See [delete a transfer](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#delete-a-transfer).

To delete several transactions at once, see [bulk delete](/transactions/search-and-filter/#delete-several-transactions).

## Keyboard shortcuts

These work while the transaction form is open:

- **Ctrl + Enter** (**⌘ + Enter** on a Mac) saves the transaction.
- **Ctrl + Shift + Enter** (**⌘ + Shift + Enter** on a Mac) creates the transaction and keeps the **Add Transaction** form you opened from the header open for the next one.
- **Alt + T** (**⌥ Option + T** on a Mac) opens or closes the templates list when you're adding an expense or income.
- **Ctrl + D** (**⌘ + D** on a Mac) duplicates the transaction you're editing, when **Duplicate** is offered.

## Good to know

- **You can't add transactions to a bank-connected account by hand.** Real transactions on those accounts come from the bank.
- **Bank-synced transactions that nothing else categorizes land in "Other".** Open them and pick a better category any time.
- **After a trial or plan ends, your data becomes read-only.** You can still look at everything, but you can't add or edit transactions until you subscribe. See [read-only mode](/settings/plans-and-billing/#read-only-mode).

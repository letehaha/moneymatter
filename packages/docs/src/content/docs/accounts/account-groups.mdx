Account groups are folders for your accounts. Put all your accounts at one bank together, or keep your savings apart from your everyday spending. Groups only change how accounts are organised on screen. They never change a balance or a statistic.

## Where groups show up

- **In the sidebar**: grouped accounts sit in a folder under **Bank Accounts**. Each collapsed folder shows the combined balance of its accounts in your base currency. Folders are sorted A–Z, and accounts without a group come after them.
- **On the Accounts page**: the groups you created that contain at least one manual account appear as folders you can expand in the **Manual accounts** section. Each shows how many accounts it holds and their combined balance. Empty groups, and groups that only hold bank-synced accounts, don't appear here.
- **In account filters**: when you filter transactions or reports by account, accounts are listed under their group, so you can tick a whole group at once.

<Aside type="note" title="Groups don't affect your stats">
Your dashboard and reports add up accounts the same way whether they're grouped or not. To keep an account out of your reports, use [Exclude from statistics](/accounts/manage-accounts/#exclude-an-account-from-statistics) instead.
</Aside>

## Create a group

<Steps>

1. Click the gear button (**Settings**) at the top right of the app.
2. In the settings menu, under **Workspace**, choose **Accounts groups**.
3. Click **Add group**.
4. Enter a **Group name**. Optionally, click the square next to it to pick a logo. Without one, the group shows a folder icon.
5. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

You can also create a group:

- from the sidebar: find the **Accounts** heading above your list of accounts (it has a gear and a **+** button). Click **+** and choose **New accounts group**
- from an account: on its **Settings** tab, click the edit button next to **Account group**, then **Create new group**

A new group stays out of the sidebar until you add an account to it.

## Add an account to a group

You add accounts from the account itself.

<Steps>

1. Open the account and select the **Settings** tab.
2. Next to **Account group**, click the edit button. The **Link account group** dialog opens.
3. Pick the group. The group the account is in now is marked **Current**.
4. Click **Save**. It appears once you've picked a different group.

</Steps>

An account can be in only one group. Picking a new group moves it out of the old one.

To take an account out of its group, choose **No group** ("Keep this account ungrouped") in the same dialog and click **Save**.

## Manage your groups

All your groups are listed in **Settings** → **Accounts groups**. Each row shows the group's logo, name, number of accounts and combined balance. Click a row to expand it.

In an expanded group you can:

- **Rename it or change its logo**: edit the **Group name** or the logo and click **Save**.
- **See its accounts** under **Accounts in this group**. Click an account to open it.
- **Remove an account from the group**: click the **Remove from group** button next to it, then **Remove**. The account isn't deleted. It only leaves the group.
- **Delete the group**: click **Delete group**, check the list of accounts that will become ungrouped, and click **Delete group** again to confirm. Your accounts are kept. They simply become ungrouped.

You can't add accounts to a group from this page. Do that [from the account](#add-an-account-to-a-group).

## Groups created by bank connections

When you connect a bank, MoneyMatter creates a group for that connection's accounts. These groups are marked **Created by bank connection** and have a **Manage connection** button.

The group doesn't represent the connection itself. You can rename it, move accounts in or out, or delete it, and your accounts keep syncing either way. See [bank connections](/import/bank-connections/).

On the **Accounts** page, bank-synced accounts are always listed under **Bank connections**, whatever group you put them in.

If you move a manual account into a group created by a bank connection, the sidebar shows it in that group's folder, but on the **Accounts** page it's listed as an ungrouped account under **Manual accounts**.

## Good to know

- **Archiving an account removes it from its group**, and unarchiving doesn't put it back. See [archive an account](/accounts/manage-accounts/#archive-an-account).
- **Groups with no active accounts don't appear in the sidebar.** They're still listed in **Settings** → **Accounts groups**.
- **Hide zero balances** in the sidebar settings also hides a group when every account in it has a balance of 0.

An account is any place your money lives: a current account, a credit card, a cash wallet, a savings pot. Every transaction you record belongs to one account. This page shows how to create a **manual account**, which is one you keep up to date yourself by adding or importing transactions.

If you'd rather have your bank send transactions automatically, see [bank connections](/import/bank-connections/). Bank connections are part of the Plus plan (see [plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/)).

## Create a manual account

<Steps>

1. In the sidebar, open **Accounts** → **All Accounts**.
2. Click **Create account** at the top of the page. On a narrow screen, click the ⋮ button (**Account actions**) first, then **Create account**.
3. Enter an **Account name**. You can also click the square next to the name to pick a logo (more on that [below](#account-name-and-logo)).
4. Choose the **Currency**.
5. Enter the **Initial balance**. Read [choose the right initial balance](#choose-the-right-initial-balance) first if you plan to add past transactions.
6. Pick an **Account Category**.
7. Leave **Credit limit** at 0 unless you know you need it (see [credit limit](#credit-limit)).
8. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

The new account appears in the sidebar and on the **Accounts** page right away.

### Other ways to open the form

- In the sidebar, find the **Accounts** heading above your list of accounts (it has a gear and a **+** button). Click **+** and choose **New account**.
- If you have no accounts yet, the **Accounts** page shows a **Create Manual Account** button.

## What each field means

### Account name and logo

The **Account name** is how the account appears everywhere in the app. Pick something you'll recognise at a glance, like "Revolut EUR" or "Wallet".

The logo is optional. Click the square next to the name and choose:

- **Brand** to search for a company logo, such as your bank's.
- **Letters** to use one or two letters on a colour you pick.

Without a logo, the account shows the icon of its category.

### Currency

The currency the account holds. The list only shows currencies you've already added. To use a new one, click **Add new currency +** at the bottom of the list, and it will be selected for you once it's added. The field starts on your base currency.

Accounts in another currency still show up in your totals. MoneyMatter converts them into your base currency using the exchange rate. See [base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/).

<Aside type="caution" title="You can't change the currency later">
Pick the currency carefully. Once the account is created, its currency is fixed. If you get it wrong, create a new account in the right currency.
</Aside>

### Initial balance

How much money was in the account before the first transaction you record in MoneyMatter. It can be negative, for example −300 for a card you already owe 300 on. You can [change it later](/accounts/manage-accounts/#change-the-initial-balance) if you get it wrong.

From then on, MoneyMatter works out the balance for you:

**Current balance = initial balance + money in − money out**

Money in and out includes everything recorded on the account: income, expenses, transfers and balance adjustments. Every time you add, edit or delete a transaction on the account, the balance updates.

You can see and [change the initial balance](/accounts/manage-accounts/#change-the-initial-balance) later on the account's **Details** tab. An import may also adjust it for you (see [below](#choose-the-right-initial-balance)). If the balance drifted from a specific date, use [Adjust balance](/accounts/manage-accounts/#adjust-a-balance) instead.

### Account Category

The category describes what kind of account this is. You can choose from:

- **General** (the default)
- **Cash**
- **Current account**
- **Credit card**
- **Saving account**
- **Bonus**
- **Insurance**
- **Investment**
- **Overdraft**
- **Crypto**

The category mostly sets the icon and the label shown in your account lists. It doesn't change the balance or which transactions count. For example, choosing **Credit card** doesn't turn on any credit features, and choosing **Investment** doesn't create an investment portfolio. One exception: the [Net Worth History](/stats/analytics-reports/#net-worth-history) report sorts debts by type, so a negative balance on a **Credit card** account counts as a **Credit card** liability, while on most other accounts it counts as **Overdraft**. You can [change the category](/accounts/manage-accounts/#change-the-category) at any time.

For savings, GICs and investment accounts your bank manages, see [savings and investment accounts](/accounts/savings-and-investment-accounts/) for how to record interest, dividends and changes in value.

Vehicles have their own form. See [vehicles](/accounts/vehicles/).

### Credit limit

For credit cards and overdrafts. Accounts with a credit limit show up in the **Credit Utilization** widget on the [dashboard](/stats/dashboard/).

Some banks show a balance that **already includes** your credit line. For example, a card with a 1,000 EUR limit where you've spent 200 EUR shows a balance of 800 EUR. In that case, enter the credit line (1,000) here. When **Include credit limits in balance** is turned on in **Settings** → **General** (see [general settings](/settings/general-settings/)), MoneyMatter subtracts the limit and shows what you actually owe: −200 EUR.

If you track the card as the amount you owe (a negative balance), you can still enter the limit, but keep **Include credit limits in balance** off. Otherwise the limit is subtracted from your balance too, and a card at −200 EUR shows −1,200 EUR. See [credit limits](/accounts/manage-accounts/#credit-limits) for the full picture.

The credit limit can't be negative, and you can change it later.

## Choose the right initial balance

What to enter depends on how you'll bring in your past transactions.

**If you'll add them by hand**, enter what the account held before your first recorded transaction, not today's balance. The balance is the initial balance plus every transaction, so each one you add moves it.

Say your bank shows 1,200 EUR today, and you're about to add last month's transactions: 800 EUR of spending and 500 EUR of income.

- If you enter 1,200 as the initial balance, those transactions move it to 900 EUR (1,200 − 800 + 500), which is wrong.
- Enter 1,500 instead (1,200 + 800 − 500). Once they're added, the balance matches your bank: 1,200 EUR.

**If you'll import them**, it depends on the **Update account balances from imported transactions** checkbox in the import:

- **Off** (the default): enter today's balance, 1,200 EUR. The import keeps it and adjusts the initial balance for you.
- **On**: imported transactions move the balance, so enter the balance before the first transaction, 1,500 EUR, as above.

See [how an import affects balances](/import/import-from-csv/#how-an-import-affects-balances). **Import from any Text Source** has no such checkbox and always moves the balance, so treat it like adding transactions by hand. See [other import sources](/import/other-import-sources/#import-from-any-text-source).

<Aside type="tip" title="Not sure of the starting number?">
Leave the initial balance at 0 and add your transactions by hand. Then [change the initial balance](/accounts/manage-accounts/#change-the-initial-balance) to your bank's balance today minus the balance MoneyMatter shows. For example, if your bank shows 1,200 EUR and MoneyMatter shows −300 EUR, set the initial balance to 1,500. The whole history moves with it, so there's no jump in your balance history.

If you're importing, enter today's balance and leave the import checkbox off, as described above.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- **You can't edit the currency.** Everything else can be changed: the name, logo, category, credit limit and, on manual accounts, the [initial balance](/accounts/manage-accounts/#change-the-initial-balance). An import may also adjust the initial balance for you so your current balance stays the same. See [how an import affects balances](/import/import-from-csv/#how-an-import-affects-balances).
- **Creating an account ticks off a task in the Quick Start checklist.** See [the Quick Start checklist](/getting-started/quick-start/#the-quick-start-checklist).
- **Keep accounts tidy with groups.** You can put related accounts into [account groups](/accounts/account-groups/).

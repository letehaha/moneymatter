Every new MoneyMatter account starts with a free 40-day trial. After that, you pick a paid plan to keep adding to your data. This page covers what each plan includes, how to subscribe or make changes, and what happens when a trial or plan runs out.

## Open Plan & billing

<Steps>

1. Click the gear button (**Settings**) at the top right of the app.
2. In the settings menu, under **Personal**, choose **Plan & billing**.

</Steps>

The **Current plan** box at the top shows where you stand: the days left in your trial, your plan and renewal date, or a **Read-only** badge (see [Read-only mode](#read-only-mode)). The plan cards are below it.

Any **See plans** link in the app also brings you here.

## Your free trial

- **40 days, no card needed.** The trial starts automatically when you sign up.
- **Almost everything is included.** You get every Plus feature except **Restore from backup**. You can still [download a backup](/settings/security-and-account/) during the trial. Restore is left out because restoring into a brand-new trial account would let anyone restart the trial forever.
- **Nothing is charged when it ends.** There's no card on file, so if you don't subscribe, your account simply becomes [read-only](#read-only-mode).
- **One trial per email address.** If you delete your account and sign up again with the same email, you continue the original 40-day trial instead of starting a new one.

A banner at the top of the app shows how many days you have left. On **Plan & billing**, a progress bar shows "Day X of 40" and changes colour when 7 or fewer days remain.

## Compare plans

There are two plans you can buy, **Essential** and **Plus**. Features not listed below work the same on both.

| Feature | Essential | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts, transactions, categories, stats | Yes | Yes |
| Imports from CSV, OFX, YNAB, Wallet and MS Money | Yes | Yes |
| Transaction attachments | Yes | Yes |
| [Download a backup and restore from backup](/settings/security-and-account/) | Yes | Yes |
| Export your data | Yes | Yes |
| [Budgeting](/budgeting/start-budgeting/) | Yes | Yes |
| [Goals](/goals/overview/) | Yes | Yes |
| [Bank connections](/import/bank-connections/) | No | Yes |
| AI features | On your own API key | On your own key, or on MoneyMatter's key (fair-use capped) |
| **Attach invoice** (AI reads an invoice and finds its transaction) | 5 free tries | Yes |
| [FIRE planner](/stats/fire-planner/) | 14-day free trial | Yes |
| [Smart tracking](/ai/ai-features/#smart-tracking-free-trial) | 14-day free trial | Yes |
| Household seats | 2 | 5 |

Household seats are how many people can join your household, not counting you. The same limit applies separately to each account you share. Only people who have accepted your invitation take up a seat. Pending invitations don't.

The third card, **Premium**, is marked **Planned** and isn't for sale yet. If you'd like it, click **Vote on the roadmap**. It costs nothing.

### Prices

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | $5 / month | $30 / year (works out to $2.50 / mo, you save $30) |
| Plus | $8 / month | $55 / year (works out to $4.58 / mo, you save $41) |

Prices are in US dollars and don't include tax. MoneyMatter's payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe's checkout page shows the price in your local currency and adds any tax that applies.

### Attach invoice free tries

**Attach invoice** is in the arrow menu next to **Add transaction** at the top of the app. You drop in an invoice or receipt, and MoneyMatter finds the transaction it belongs to. Plus includes it. Without Plus, you get **5 free tries in total**, and the dialog shows how many are left.

A try only counts once your invoice has been read and your transactions have been searched. An unsupported file or a document that isn't an invoice doesn't use one up.

### FIRE planner free trial

The [FIRE planner](/stats/fire-planner/) is in **Analytics** under **Investments**. Plus includes it, and so does the 40-day trial. Without Plus, opening the planner for the first time starts a free 14-day trial of it, once per account. When the 14 days are over, the planner and its dashboard widget are locked until you move to Plus. Read-only accounts can't start this trial.

### Goals free trial

[Goals](/goals/overview/) are part of Essential and Plus, and of the 40-day trial. The Early Adopter plan doesn't include them. Instead, the **Goals** page offers **Start free trial**, a free 14-day trial of goals, once per account. When it ends, your goals stay visible, but you can't create, edit or delete them until you subscribe. Read-only accounts can't start this trial.

### Early Adopter plan

Some early users have the **Early Adopter** plan. It's a lifetime plan that can't be bought, and it shows as "Early Adopter · lifetime" with no renewal and no card on file.

- It includes everything Plus had when paid plans launched. Features added to Plus after that aren't included. So far these are **Attach invoice**, which gives you the same 5 free tries as Essential, the **FIRE planner**, which gives you the same 14-day trial, [**Smart tracking**](/ai/ai-features/#smart-tracking-free-trial), which gives you a 14-day free trial from when you first turn it on, **budgeting**, which you can try for 40 days from your first budget (see [Start budgeting](/budgeting/start-budgeting/#early-adopters)), and **goals**, which give you a 14-day free trial you start from the **Goals** page (see [Goals free trial](#goals-free-trial)).
- You get **50% off any paid plan for life**, applied automatically at checkout. If you buy Plus, you keep your lifetime plan and also get the newer Plus features.

## Subscribe to a plan

<Steps>

1. Open **Plan & billing**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** above the plan cards. The page starts on **Yearly**.
3. On the plan you want, click **Get Essential** or **Get Plus**. If you had a plan before, its card says **Subscribe again**.
4. Complete the payment on Stripe's checkout page.
5. You're taken back to **Plan & billing**. It shows **Activating your plan** for a few seconds, then "Your plan is now active."

</Steps>

If you close the checkout page without paying, nothing changes.

If activation takes longer than usual, you'll see "Your payment went through but the plan is still being set up." Refresh the page after a minute. If your plan still doesn't show, click **Send feedback** at the top of the app and tell us what happened.

<Aside type="caution" title="Subscribing during the trial starts billing today">
The plan cards say "Billed today. Cancel anytime." for a reason: your paid period starts the moment you subscribe, and the days left in your trial aren't added on top. If that subscription later ends while your original 40 days are still running, you go back to the trial for the days that remain.

Your plan also replaces the trial from that moment. If you subscribe to Essential during the trial, Plus-only features such as bank connections stop working right away, even if trial days are left.
</Aside>

## Change or cancel your subscription

Once you've subscribed, you make every change in Stripe's billing portal. Click **Manage billing** in the **Current plan** box to open it. If your last payment failed, the button says **Update payment method** and opens the same portal. That's where you update your payment method, see your invoices and cancel. When you're done, the portal sends you back to **Plan & billing**.

You can have only one subscription at a time, so once you're subscribed the other plan's card offers **Switch to ...** instead of **Get ...**.

### Switch plan or billing cycle

- **Change plan:** on the other plan's card, click **Switch to Plus** or **Switch to Essential**.
- **Change billing cycle:** select the other cycle in the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle, then click **Switch to Yearly billing** or **Switch to Monthly billing** on your plan's card.

Both buttons open the billing portal, where you confirm the change.

<Aside type="note" title="Good to know">
The **Your plan** label only appears on the card for the exact plan and cycle you pay for. If you pay monthly and flip the toggle to **Yearly**, your plan's card offers **Switch to Yearly billing** instead.
</Aside>

### Cancel

<Steps>

1. Open **Plan & billing** and click **Manage billing**.
2. Cancel your subscription in the billing portal.

</Steps>

Cancelling doesn't cut you off straight away. You keep full access until the end of the period you've paid for, and the **Current plan** box shows "Access until [date], then read-only" and "No further charges". Changed your mind? Renew in the billing portal before that date, and your renewal date stays the same.

### If a payment fails

On **Plan & billing**, the **Current plan** box shows a **Payment failed** badge and "Access continues until [date]". Stripe retries your card automatically. To fix it, click **Update payment method** in the **Current plan** box to open the billing portal. The **Update your payment method** link in the banner at the top of the app brings you to this page. If Stripe still can't charge your card after its retries, your subscription ends and your account becomes [read-only](#read-only-mode). This can happen before the date shown.

### Paused subscriptions

While a subscription is paused, your account is [read-only](#read-only-mode), unless your 40-day trial is still running. The **Current plan** box shows a **Paused** badge and the date it resumes automatically. To review or cancel the subscription, click **Manage billing** to open the billing portal.

## Read-only mode

Your account becomes read-only when your trial ends without a subscription, when your subscription ends, or while it's paused (unless your 40-day trial is still running). **Nothing is deleted.** All your data stays exactly where it was, and it's all editable again as soon as you pick a plan.

**In read-only mode you can:**

- View all your accounts, transactions and stats.
- Export your data: go to **Settings** → **Import/Export** → **Export**.
- View and download attachments already added to your transactions.
- Subscribe to a plan or open the billing portal.
- Delete your account.

**You can't:**

- Add, edit or delete your data, such as accounts, transactions or categories. (Most of your preferences under **Appearance**, **Language** and **General** can still be changed. The **Quick Start guide** toggle can't, and neither can currency or AI settings.)
- Download a backup. Use **Export** to take your data with you instead.
- Connect a bank or sync your bank connections.

The app doesn't hide edit buttons in read-only mode. When you try to save a change, a message stays on screen, for example "Your trial has ended. Subscribe to keep editing your data.", with a **See plans** button that takes you to **Plan & billing**.

## When a feature isn't in your plan

If you open something your plan doesn't include, you'll see a lock card that says **Included in the Plus plan** (or **Included in the Essential plan**) with a **See plans** button. For example, Essential users see it when they start adding a [bank connection](/import/bank-connections/). They can still see bank connections they already have, but can't add, reconnect, sync, edit or disconnect them.

During the trial, the **Select backup file** button under **Restore from backup** is turned off, because restore isn't part of the trial.

## Good to know

- **Everyone needs their own plan to make changes.** If you share data with someone, whether they can edit depends on their own trial or plan, not yours.
- **Deleting your account cancels your subscription first.** If the cancellation doesn't go through, your account isn't deleted, so you're never left paying for an account you can't reach. Try again, or cancel from the billing portal first. See [Security and account](/settings/security-and-account/).
- **A full refund ends the subscription.** If the payment for your current period is fully refunded, the subscription ends right away.

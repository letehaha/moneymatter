You can give other MoneyMatter users access to your money. Each person signs in with their own login and keeps their own data. They see only what you share with them.

## What you can share

| What | Who can see it | Where you manage it |
|---|---|---|
| **One account** | The people you invite to that account | The account's **Sharing** tab |
| **Your household** | Your household members. They get every account you own, including accounts you add later. | **Settings** → **Household** |
| **One tracker** | The people you invite to that tracker | The tracker's **Share** button |

A household covers accounts only. Portfolios, ventures, trackers and [budgets](/budgeting/start-budgeting/) aren't included. To share a tracker with a household member, [share the tracker](/planned/trackers/#share-a-tracker) on its own.

You can't share a [budget](/budgeting/start-budgeting/) at all, and a budget can't use accounts that someone shared with you.

## Roles

Each person you invite gets one role. The role names differ a little from screen to screen.

| Role | What the person can do | Share account dialog | Account **Sharing** tab and **Household** | Tracker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Read | See the account or tracker and its transactions | **Read-only** | **Read** | **View only** |
| Write | Read, plus add transactions. On a tracker, add their own transactions to it. | **Read & add transactions** | **Write** | **Attach own transactions** |
| Manage | Write, plus change other people's roles and remove them | **Full access** | **Manage** | **Manage** |

Household members can only have **Read** or **Write**.

On an account or a household, a Write or Manage role also has a scope:

- **All transactions on the account** (on the account's **Sharing** tab, **All transactions**; on the household page, **Can edit any transaction**): they can edit and delete every transaction on the account.
- **Only transactions they created** (on the account's **Sharing** tab, **Own transactions only**; on the household page, **Can edit only their own transactions**): they can edit and delete only the transactions they added themselves.

A tracker has no scope. A Write member can add and remove only their own transactions.

Only you, the owner, can:

- send, resend and cancel invitations
- rename, edit, archive or delete the account
- delete the tracker

A Manage member of a tracker can also edit the tracker's settings and archive it.

If you share one of your accounts with a household member, that account share wins over their household role for that account. It works the other way round too: when someone joins your household, the accounts and trackers you had shared with them one by one are replaced by the household access, and your pending invitations to them for single accounts or trackers are cancelled. Trackers aren't part of a household, so share them again afterwards.

## Share one account

<Steps>

1. Open the account.
2. Click the **Account actions** button (three dots) next to the account name and choose **Share account**. You can also open the account's **Sharing** tab and click **Invite another**.
3. Enter the person's email under **Recipient email**.
4. Choose a **Permission**. For write access, also choose a **Transaction edit scope**.
5. Click **Send invitation**.

</Steps>

## Invite people to your household

<Steps>

1. Click the gear button (**Settings**) at the top right of the app.
2. Under **Workspace**, choose **Household**.
3. Under **My household**, enter the person's **Email**, choose a **Permission** and, for **Write**, a **Write scope**. Write is selected by default.
4. Click **Send household invite**.

</Steps>

Your members are listed under **My household**. Use **Make read-only** or **Allow writes** to change a member's access, and **Remove** to take it away. Pending invitations sit under the members with a **Pending** badge.

## Share a tracker

<Steps>

1. Open the tracker under **Planning** → **Trackers**.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Click **Invite someone**, enter the email, choose a **Permission** and click **Send invitation**.

</Steps>

The same window lists the tracker's members and pending invitations. What each permission lets people do is described in [Trackers](/planned/trackers/#share-a-tracker).

## Invitations

- **Who you can invite.** Any email address, including people who don't have a MoneyMatter account yet. They can sign up with that address and then accept. You can't invite yourself.
- **How they hear about it.** They get an email with a link. If they already have an account, they also get an in-app notification.
- **Copy invite link.** Each pending invitation has a **Copy invite link** button, so you can send the link yourself. The link only works for the invited email address. On a server without email sending set up, the invitation shows "Email sending not configured — share the invite link manually".
- **Expiry.** An invitation expires after 7 days. You get a notification when it does.
- **Resend.** **Resend** sends a fresh email with a new link and restarts the 7 days. Any older link stops working. You can resend the same invitation up to 3 times in 24 hours. The button only appears when the server can send email.
- **Cancel.** **Cancel** (or **Cancel invitation**) withdraws it, so the link stops working.
- **Limits.** You can have up to 10 pending invitations for each account, household or tracker, and send up to 30 invitations a day in total.

Pending invitations don't use up a seat. See [Seats](#seats).

## Accept or decline an invitation

You can open an invitation in three ways:

- click the link in the email
- click the notification in the app
- go to **Settings** → **Shared with me** and click **Review** under **Pending invitations**

A dialog shows who is sharing what, the **Access level** and when the invitation **Expires**. Click **Accept** or **Decline**. The person who invited you is told either way.

### Your base currency must match

You can accept only if your base currency is the same as the owner's. Otherwise the dialog says "Base currency doesn't match" and offers **Update base currency**.

While you share anything with anyone, in either direction, you can't change your base currency. A budget also blocks the change. See [Base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/).

### Share your household back

When you join someone's household, the dialog asks if you want to share your own household with them too. Pick their permission and click **Share back**, or click **Not now**.

## When something is shared with you

### Where you find it

- **Settings** → **Shared with me** lists everything you have access to, grouped into **Accounts**, **Households** and **Trackers shared with you**. Each row has a **Leave** button.
- On the **Accounts** page, accounts other people shared with you are in the **Shared with me** section.
- In the sidebar and on the account page, a shared account shows "Shared by @name" or "Shared via @name's household", with your role.
- A shared tracker appears in your tracker list with a "Shared by @name" label.

### What you can and can't do on a shared account

- You can put a shared account into one of your own [account groups](/accounts/account-groups/). This doesn't change the owner's groups.
- With write access, you add transactions with the owner's categories and payees, not your own.
- You can send money from your own account into a shared account you can add transactions to. Both sides then show the transfer. See [transfers to a shared account](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/#transfers-to-a-shared-account).
- You can't start a transfer from a shared account. You also can't delete the shared account's side of a transfer, only the owner can. Deleting your own side removes both sides.
- You can't add planned transactions or link refunds on a shared account, and you can't move a transaction from it to another account.
- The account's settings, bank connection and balance adjustments are the owner's. You can't change them. Inviting people is owner-only too, but a Manage member can change other members' roles or remove them on the **Sharing** tab.

On a shared tracker with write access, you can add and remove your own transactions. The tracker's totals include them.

### How shared data counts in your stats

A shared account counts in full for you, including the owner's transactions:

- the dashboard's expenses, cash flow and balance history
- the [pivot report](/stats/analytics-reports/)
- your total balance on the dashboard

There are two exceptions:

- **Net worth** counts only what you own. A shared account isn't in your net worth history.
- **Planned transactions** the owner adds to a shared account don't show up in your stats.

The overview at the top of the **Accounts** page counts your own accounts, not the ones in the **Shared with me** section.

## Leave or remove someone

**To stop sharing**, remove the person:

- an account: the trash button on the account's **Sharing** tab
- your household: **Remove** on **Settings** → **Household**
- a tracker: **Revoke access** in the tracker's **Share** window

**To give up access** someone gave you, click **Leave** on **Settings** → **Shared with me**. For a household, you can also use **Leave** under **Households I'm in** on the **Household** page.

The other person always finds out. When you remove someone, they get an in-app notification and an email. When you leave, the owner gets an in-app notification and an email.

What happens to the data:

- **Transactions stay on the account they were added to.** Transactions someone added to your account stay on your account after they leave.
- **Transfers between household members are split.** When a household membership ends, each transfer between the two of you becomes two separate Out of Wallet transactions, one on each side. Balances don't change, and each note gets a line such as "Transfer to @name • Account name". The same happens when either account in such a transfer is deleted.
- **Trackers lose the member's transactions.** When someone leaves a tracker or is removed, the transactions they added come off the tracker. The transactions themselves stay in their account.

If the owner deletes a shared account or tracker, everyone with access gets a notification.

## Notifications

| Event | Who is notified |
|---|---|
| You're invited | The invited person, by email and, if they have an account, in the app |
| An invitation is accepted, declined or expires | The owner |
| The invitation email couldn't be sent | The owner |
| A member is removed | The removed person, in the app and by email |
| A member leaves | The owner, in the app and by email |
| A household member's access changes between Read and Write | That member |
| The owner deletes a shared account or tracker | Everyone who had access |
| A household member deletes their MoneyMatter account | The household owner |

Notifications appear in the bell at the top of the app. See [Notifications](/settings/notifications/).

## Seats

Your plan sets how many people can have access to each thing you share, not counting you. The limit applies to your household, and separately to each account and each tracker you share.

| Plan | Seats |
|---|---|
| Essential | 2 |
| Plus | 5 |
| Early Adopter | 5 |
| Self-hosted | 10 |

The trial gives you Plus seats. Only people who have accepted count. If the limit is reached, you can't send another invitation, and nobody else can accept one until a seat frees up. If your plan changes to one with fewer seats, people who already have access keep it.

## Good to know

- **Everyone needs their own plan to make changes.** Whether someone can add transactions to your account depends on their own trial or plan, not yours. In [read-only mode](/settings/plans-and-billing/#read-only-mode) you can't invite, accept, decline, leave or remove anyone.
- **Demo accounts can't share.**
- **Wiping your data or deleting your account ends all sharing**, both what you shared and what was shared with you. People you shared accounts or your household with are notified. See [Security and account](/settings/security-and-account/).

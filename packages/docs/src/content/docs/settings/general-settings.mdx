This page walks through the options on the **General**, **Appearance** and **Language** settings pages and explains what each one changes in the rest of the app. At the end there's a short map of the other settings areas.

## Open Settings

<Steps>

1. Click the gear button (**Settings**) at the top right of the app.
2. Pick a page from the settings menu. **Appearance**, **Language** and **General** are under **Personal**.

</Steps>

On a narrow screen, Settings first shows the list of pages. Use **Back to Settings** to return to it.

There's no Save button on these pages. Every change is saved as soon as you make it.

<Aside type="note" title="Which settings follow you to other devices">
Almost everything here is saved to your account, so it's the same on every device you sign in on. The two exceptions are **Theme** and **Language**: they're remembered by the browser you set them in.
</Aside>

### Theme

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. **System** follows your device's light or dark mode and switches along with it. **System** is the default.

Your choice is remembered by this browser only, so a new browser or device starts on **System**.

### Sidebar sections

Choose which sections appear in the sidebar. **Bank Accounts** is always shown. You can hide **Portfolios**, **Ventures**, **Cars** and **Loans** if you don't use them. All of them are shown by default.

You can also change this from the sidebar itself: click the small gear button next to the **Accounts** heading in the sidebar (not the **Settings** button at the top right). That menu also has **Hide zero balances**, which hides accounts with a zero balance, and any group whose accounts are all at zero. When [**Include credit limits in balance**](#include-credit-limits-in-balance) is on, it checks the balance after the credit limit is subtracted.

### Currency symbols

Turn on **Use short currency symbols** to show amounts with short symbols like Rp, ₴ or zł instead of IDR, UAH or PLN. It changes how every amount in the app is displayed.

The catch is that currencies sharing a symbol all look the same: USD, CAD, AUD and SGD all become $. If you hold several of those, leave this off.

## Language

Pick the language for the app. It switches right away.

- **English** and **Українська** are maintained by the MoneyMatter team.
- **Español**, **Bahasa**, **Русский**, **Slovenčina**, **Deutsch**, **Français** and **Português (Brasil)** are under **Community translations**. Volunteers translate them on Crowdin, and anything not translated yet is shown in English.

To fix a translation or help with a language, click **Contribute on Crowdin** in the **Missing or wrong translations?** box. You can also request a new language from the feature board linked there.

Like the theme, your language is remembered by the browser. On a new browser or device, MoneyMatter starts in your browser's language if it's supported, otherwise in English. You can also pick a language on the sign-in and sign-up screens.

## General

The **General** page controls how your numbers are calculated and what forms show you.

### Include credit limits in balance

Off by default. When it's on, the credit limit of each credit account is subtracted from its balance. You see how much you actually owe instead of how much credit you have left.

Turn this on if you record a card's balance the way your bank shows it, as the credit you have left. The limit itself is set on each account; see [credit limits](/accounts/manage-accounts/#credit-limits).

For example, a credit card with a 1,000 EUR limit shows a balance of 700 EUR, meaning 700 EUR is still available to spend. With this setting on, it counts as **−300 EUR**, the amount you've actually spent.

This changes more than charts:

- **Stats:** the dashboard's **Balance trend** and **Net Worth** widgets, and the **Net Worth History** report in Analytics.
- **Displayed balances:** the account list in the sidebar, the **Accounts** page, the account's own page, and account group totals.

Only accounts with a credit limit above 0 are affected. In stats, the limit isn't subtracted for loans, cars, or accounts with [**Exclude from statistics**](/accounts/manage-accounts/#exclude-an-account-from-statistics) turned on.

### Count categories as savings

Choose categories that represent money you're setting aside, such as a "Savings" or "Emergency fund" category. Transactions in these categories, and in all their subcategories, drop out of cash-flow totals. That money then counts toward your monthly savings and savings rate instead of your expenses.

For example, each month you pay 500 EUR into a savings plan you don't track in MoneyMatter and record it in your "Savings" category. Normally that's 500 EUR of expenses. With "Savings" chosen here, it leaves your expenses, so your net cash flow goes up by 500 EUR and your **Savings Rate** rises to match. If the money goes to one of your MoneyMatter accounts, record a [transfer](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/) instead: transfers never count as expenses.

- It affects cash-flow views only: the **Cash Flow** report in Analytics, the dashboard's **Cash Flow** widget, and the **Monthly Comparison** chart in **Trends Comparison**.
- Other reports, like spending by category, still show these categories.
- It applies to income in those categories too, not just spending.
- Subcategories you create later are covered automatically.

See [how stats are calculated](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/) for more on what counts where.

### Tax categories

Choose the categories you record taxes in, such as "Taxes" or "Social security". Subcategories are included, and subcategories you create later are covered automatically.

- In the money flow diagram of the [Cash Flow report](/stats/analytics-reports/#where-the-money-went), spending in these categories shows as **Taxes**, separate from your expenses.
- The [**Average tax rate**](/stats/dashboard/#average-tax-rate) dashboard widget uses them to work out how much of your income goes to taxes.
- Everywhere else, including the Cash Flow chart and widget, taxes still count as expenses.
- Only spending moves to **Taxes**. A tax refund still counts as income.
- A category chosen in both **Count categories as savings** and **Tax categories** counts as savings.

### Default account

The account that's already selected when you create a transaction. If you're on an account's page, that account is selected instead.

With no default set, the first active account is used.

### Show archived accounts

Off by default. When it's off, archived accounts are hidden from account pickers, such as the one in the transaction form. An archived account that's already selected, for example on a transaction you're editing, is still shown.

If your default account is archived, it's ignored while this setting is off.

### Quick Start guide

Shows the Quick Start checklist: a panel on the right edge of the app on desktop, or a floating button on mobile. Turn it off to hide the checklist everywhere. You can turn it back on at any time. See [the Quick Start checklist](/getting-started/quick-start/#the-quick-start-checklist).

### Show upcoming transactions

On by default. Pins a section at the top of the transactions list with scheduled payments that are overdue or due within the next 3 days.

### Optional transaction fields

Choose which extra fields appear in the transaction form. A field that already has a value on a transaction is always shown, even if you turn it off here.

| Field | What it's for | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **External URL** | A link to an order page, booking confirmation or receipt. | Off |
| **External reference** | An order number, invoice number or booking reference. | Off |
| **Original amount** | The amount actually charged in another currency, when it differs from the account's currency. | On |
| **Location** | Where the purchase happened. Fill it from your device's location or type the coordinates in. | Off |

**Map picker** sits under **Location** and only works when **Location** is on. It lets you pick a place on a map and search for addresses. It's off by default for privacy: the map and address search come from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address when the map opens. If it's off, the transaction form asks you to allow the map the first time you click **Pick on map**.

### Bank sync options <Badge text="Plus" variant="tip" />

On the **General** page, these two switches come right after **Include credit limits in balance**.

They only matter if you use [bank connections](/import/bank-connections/), which are part of the Plus plan. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/).

#### Match transfers with manual accounts

Off by default. When your bank syncs, MoneyMatter looks for [transfers](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/) between your accounts. Normally it only pairs transactions from two bank-connected accounts. Turn this on to let it pair a synced bank transaction with one you entered by hand.

It's off by default because a matched transaction becomes one side of a transfer, and transfers have no category. So a manual transaction you'd categorized drops out of your category stats once it's matched.

#### Import pending bank transactions

Off by default. It only affects banks you connected through the Enable Banking provider. When it's on, transactions are saved before the bank books them. They're marked as pending, never change the account balance, and often lack merchant details until they're booked.

Other connections work the same either way: SimpleFIN and Lunch Flow always import booked transactions only, and Monobank imports every transaction as a regular one.

## Other settings areas

The settings menu has more pages. Here's what each one is for.

**Personal**

- **Security**: sign-in methods, active sessions, password, backups, and deleting your data or account. See [Security and account](/settings/security-and-account/).
- **Plan & billing**: choose a plan and manage your billing. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/).

**Workspace**

- **Currencies**: your base currency, the other currencies you use, and exchange rates. See [Base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/).
- **Categories**: organize transactions into categories and subcategories. See [Categories](/transactions/categories/).
- **Tags**: manage the tags you add to transactions. See [Tags](/transactions/tags/).
- **Payees**: the list of merchants you transact with. See [Payees](/transactions/payees/).
- **Accounts groups**: organize accounts into groups. See [Account groups](/accounts/account-groups/).
- **Import/Export**: bring in data from files and other apps, or download your data as a zip for spreadsheets, taxes or moving elsewhere. See [Import from CSV](/import/import-from-csv/) and [Other import sources](/import/other-import-sources/).
- **AI**: pick which AI model powers each feature, add your own models, and connect AI apps to your data.
- **Shared with me**: data other people have shared with you.
- **Household**: share your data with other people.

## Good to know

- **Most settings still work in read-only mode.** If your trial or plan has ended, you can still change the options on these pages, except the **Quick Start guide** switch. See [read-only mode](/settings/plans-and-billing/#read-only-mode).
- **Two settings change which transactions count as spending**: **Count categories as savings**, and **Match transfers with manual accounts**, which can turn a categorized transaction into a transfer.

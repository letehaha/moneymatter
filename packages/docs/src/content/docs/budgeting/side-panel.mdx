The side panel next to the budget explains the numbers in the grid. With no line selected it covers the whole period. Click a line and it switches to that line.

## Open the side panel

- **On a wide screen**, the panel is always shown to the right of the grid.
- **On a narrower screen**, click **Summary** at the top of the budget. The panel slides in from the right.

The panel starts on **Period overview**, with the dates of the period you're viewing. To see one line:

<Steps>

1. Click the line's name in the grid. On a narrow screen, the panel opens with the line's details.
2. To go back to the overview, click the line again, click the **X** (**Close details**) at the top of the panel, or press Esc.

</Steps>

Every section folds away with a click on its title. The panel remembers which sections you folded, in this browser.

## What each view shows

| Section | Period overview | Line details |
|---|---|---|
| [Breakdown](#breakdown) | Yes | Yes |
| [Goals](#goals) | When any line has a goal | No |
| [Scheduled](#scheduled) | Current and later periods | Current and later periods, when the line has something scheduled |
| [Assigned vs spent](#assigned-vs-spent) | Yes | Yes |
| [Spending pace](#spending-pace) | Current period only | Current period only |
| [Goal](#goal) | No | Yes |

Line details also start with the line's **Available** amount in large type. If the line's goal needs money, a note below it says how much, for example "Need $150.00 this month", with "Assign this amount here to stay on track."

## Breakdown

How the period's **Available** amount is made up: **Carried over** from the last period, plus **Assigned**, plus **Activity**, equals **Available**. In the overview it adds up every line. For a line, it's that line alone.

See [How Available is worked out](/budgeting/assigning-and-rollover/#how-available-is-worked-out).

## Goals

A summary of every goal in the period, shown when at least one line has a goal.

- **Period targets**: what all your goals call for in this period.
- **Still needed**: what they still ask for after what you've assigned.
- A badge says how many goals still need money ("X to fund"), or **All funded**.
- Below, every goal that still needs money is listed with its amount. Click one to open that line's details.

## Scheduled

What's still coming in the period, from today until the period ends:

- Recurring payments, such as subscriptions and bills, that are paid from one of the budget's accounts, or that have no account but whose category is in one of your lines.
- Planned transactions on the budget's accounts. See [Planned transactions](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/#planned-transactions).

The section shows the date of the next one and how many there are, totals for **Outflows** and **Inflows**, and the list with each item's date. A repeat icon marks a recurring payment. In line details, it only lists what falls into that line's categories.

Past periods don't show this section, and neither does a line with nothing scheduled.

## Assigned vs spent

One chart over the last 6 periods, up to the one you're viewing. Each period has a wide pale column for what was **Assigned** and a narrower bar inside it for what was **Spent**. All periods share one scale, so a period with a bigger budget draws a taller column. When spending leaves a line with a negative **Available**, that part of the bar is red and counts as **Over**. Money carried over from earlier periods covers spending too, so a bar can rise above its column without turning red.

Hover a period to see its assigned, spent and over amounts.

Under the chart you'll see the viewed period's spending out of what was assigned, with the share used, and the share used across up to 5 earlier periods, for example "avg 92% used".

## Spending pace

Shown for the current period only. It compares two bars:

- **Spent**: how much of the money you had this period is gone. That's spending measured against the carried-over money plus **Assigned**. It turns red past 100%.
- **Period elapsed**: how much of the period has passed. Today counts as passed.

If **Spent** runs well ahead of **Period elapsed**, you're spending faster than the period is going. Below the bars you'll see "X of Y" and how many days are left, for example "9d left".

## Goal

In line details, this section shows the line's goal, or **No goal set** with a **Create goal** button.

With a goal, you'll see:

- the goal and how often it repeats,
- buttons to snooze the goal for this period, **Edit goal** and **Delete goal**,
- a progress bar with "X of Y (Z%)", the status, and how much is still to go,
- **Budget X**, when the goal needs money,
- **Funding plan**, for **Yearly** and **By date** goals: how much to assign in each period until the due date, and what's left after each one.

See [Goals](/budgeting/goals/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Use it to plan the period">
Start a period in the overview: **Goals** tells you what your goals need, and **Scheduled** shows the bills still to come. Then click lines one by one to see which ones are running ahead of the period in **Spending pace**.
</Aside>

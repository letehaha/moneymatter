Each holding in a portfolio has its own page. To open it, click the holding's symbol or its **›** button (**Open holding**) on the **Holdings** tab. A trade in **Activity**, a row in the Analytics **Performance** table and a held security on the watchlist also lead here.

The header shows the symbol, its exchange and currency, and the security's name. The arrow at the top left takes you back to the portfolio.

## Price chart

The chart shows the security's daily closing price, in its own currency. Above it are the holding's **Market value** and its **unrealized** gain or loss. Pick a period: **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **YTD**, **1Y** or **All**. The period is remembered in this browser.

- Markers on the price line show your **Buys** (round, in the income colour) and **Sells** (square, in the expense colour). Trades on the same day share one marker. Hover it to see each trade's shares and price, that day's close and the total amount.
- A dashed line marks your average cost, labelled "Avg cost" with the price.

The buttons under the chart turn **Buys**, **Sells** and **Avg cost** on and off.

## Stats

Below the chart:

| Stat | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Shares** | How many you hold, and how many open lots they're in. |
| **Avg cost** | Cost basis divided by shares. |
| **Cost basis** | What the shares you hold cost you. |
| **Unrealized** | Market value minus cost basis, with the percentage. |
| **Realized** | Gain or loss from what you've sold. |
| **Dividends** | Dividends you've recorded for this holding. |
| **Weight** | The holding's share of the portfolio's holdings value, without cash. |
| **First purchase** | The date of your first buy. |

## Your timing

The **Your timing** card compares your average buy price with the average daily close since your first buy. For example: "Your average buy price $182.40 is 4.1% below the average daily close since your first buy on Mar 3, 2025 ($190.20)." Below the average means you've tended to buy on cheaper days.

The card only shows when there's price history from around your first buy onwards, and at least five closes.

## Transactions and open lots

At the bottom, two tabs:

- **Transactions**: every transaction for this holding.
- **Open lots**: the shares you still hold, by purchase. Lots are matched **first in, first out**: a sale uses up your oldest shares first. Each lot shows when you bought it (**Purchased**), **Shares**, **Cost per share** (including that buy's fees), **Current value** and **Gain**.

The **Cost basis** stat uses your average cost, so after a sale the lot totals can differ from it.

## Add a transaction

Click **Add transaction** at the top of the holding page to record a buy, sell, dividend or other transaction for this holding. The same form opens from the **+** button on the holding's row in the **Holdings** tab.

## Delete a holding

Open the ⋯ menu at the top right and choose **Delete holding**. This permanently deletes the holding and all of its transactions, and can't be undone. Afterwards you're taken back to the portfolio.

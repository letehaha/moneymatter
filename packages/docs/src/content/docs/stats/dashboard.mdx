The dashboard is your overview: balances, spending and savings for the period you choose, laid out the way you like. Open it from **Dashboard** in the sidebar.

Until you add your first account, the dashboard shows setup steps instead of widgets.

## Choose the period

The dashboard opens on the current month. Every widget that shows a period follows the date selector at the top of the dashboard (on a phone, it's pinned to the bottom of the screen).

Use the arrows on either side of the date to move the whole range back or forward by its own length. For a single month that's one month; for **Last 3 Months** it's three months. A range that doesn't cover whole months moves by the same number of days.

To pick a different range:

<Steps>

1. Click the date.
2. Choose a preset (**Current Month**, **Last 3 Months**, **Last 6 Months**, **Last 12 Months**, **This Year** or **Previous Year**), which applies straight away. Or choose **Day**, **Month**, **Quarter**, **Half-year** or **Year** and select a range.
3. For a custom range, click **Apply**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/stats/dashboard-period-selector.png"

  hint="On /dashboard, click the date button between the arrows so the picker opens. The Quick select presets (Current Month … Previous Year) and the period type tabs must be visible. Crop to the picker popover."

<Aside type="note" title="Good to know">
- The forward arrow is disabled once the period reaches the current month.
- A custom period is kept in the page address, so reloading the page keeps it.
- **Latest Transactions**, **Credit Utilization** and **FIRE progress** don't follow the period. They always show your latest data. **Budget** always shows the current budget period. **Trackers** shows each tracker's own dates. **Goals** shows where each goal stands today.
</Aside>

## Customize the layout

You choose which widgets appear, their size and their order.

<Steps>

1. Click **Customize** at the top of the dashboard. On a phone, the button is above the widgets.
2. To reorder, drag a widget by the dotted grip icon (⋮⋮) that appears on it.
3. To resize, click one of the size buttons on the widget, such as **1×1** or **2×1**. Each widget offers the sizes that suit it.
4. To remove a widget, click the red ✕ on it. You can't remove the last widget.
5. To add a widget, click it in the **Add widgets** panel. The panel lists only widgets that aren't on your dashboard yet, since each widget can appear once.
6. Click **Done** to save your layout, or **Cancel** to discard the changes.

</Steps>

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/stats/dashboard-customize-mode.png"
  alt="The dashboard in Customize mode, with drag handles, size buttons and remove buttons on each widget, the Add widgets panel, and Done and Cancel buttons."
  hint="On /dashboard, click Customize. Leave at least two widgets off the dashboard so the Add widgets panel lists them. Capture the grid with the drag handles, size buttons and red ✕ visible, plus the Done and Cancel buttons."

<Aside type="note" title="Good to know">
- Your layout is saved to your MoneyMatter account, not to the browser, so it's the same on every device.
- Widget settings (the gear icon on a widget) save as soon as you change them. You don't need to click **Customize** first.
- On a dashboard you've never customized, changes to the **Balance trend** and **Latest Transactions** settings aren't kept. Click **Customize**, then **Done**, once to save your layout, and they'll stick from then on.
- A new dashboard starts with **Balance trend**, **Latest Transactions**, **Subscriptions & Bills** and **Expenses Structure**.
</Aside>

## The widgets

The **Add widgets** panel and the widget itself sometimes use different names. Here's how they match up.

| In **Add widgets** | On the dashboard | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Balance Trend** | **Balance trend** | Your total balance over the period |
| **Spending by Categories** | **Expenses Structure** | Your spending split by category |
| **Cash Flow** | **Cash Flow** | Income, expenses and how much you saved |
| **Categories Watchlist** | **Categories Watchlist** | Chosen categories and what came in or went out of each |
| **Net Worth** | **Net Worth** | How your net worth changed over the period |
| **Credit Utilization** | **Credit Utilization** | How much of your credit limits you're using |
| **Latest Records** | **Latest Transactions** | Your most recent transactions |
| **FIRE progress** | **FIRE progress** | How close you are to financial independence (Plus) |
| **Budget** | **Budget** | What's left in the current budget period, line by line. Use **Sort by** in the gear menu to rank lines by **Highest spend first**, **Lowest spend first** or **Most overspent first** (least left at the top); **Custom order** keeps your plan's order |
| **Trackers** | **Trackers** | Each active tracker with how much of its limit is used and its net |
| **Average tax rate** | **Average tax rate** | The share of your income paid in taxes, with a 12-month trend |
| **Goals** | **Goals** | Progress toward your savings goals |

**Subscriptions & Bills** and **Planned overview** are also available as widgets.

The numbers follow the rules in [How stats are calculated](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/). Transfers between your own accounts (except loan payments), **Out of Wallet** transfers and balance adjustments never count as income or expenses. Accounts with **Exclude from statistics** on are left out of every widget's numbers. Their transactions still show in **Latest Transactions**.

### Balance trend

A chart of your balance across the period. The big number is your latest balance in the period. It's labelled **Today** when you're viewing the current month. Next to it you'll see the change **vs previous period**, compared with the same number of days just before.

Use the line selector on the widget to switch between **Total**, **Accounts** and **Portfolios**, plus **Ventures**, **Vehicles** and **Loans** when you have them. Real data stops at today.

The gear icon opens **Settings**:

- **Spike markers**: highlight days when your balance changed a lot. Set **Min change %**, **Min change $** and **Max markers**, or click **Reset defaults**, then **Accept**. Click a marker on the chart to see the transactions behind it, with a **See all transactions** link.
- **Include in total**: choose whether **Vehicles**, **Ventures** and **Loans** count toward the **Total** line. All three are on by default. With **Loans** on, what you owe on loans is subtracted from the total.
- **Fit to latest data**: when on, the chart stops at your latest data point. When off, it spans the whole period. While the planned-transactions projection is showing, this switch is disabled and the chart always runs to the end of the period. Turn off **Include planned transactions** to change it.
- **Include planned transactions** (on by default): when the period reaches today and the line is **Total** or **Accounts**, a dashed line continues past today to show where your upcoming [planned transactions](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/#planned-transactions) would take the balance.

### Expenses Structure

A donut chart of your spending by top-level category. Subcategories are grouped under their parent. The big number is your total spending for the period, with a percentage **vs previous period**.

Click a slice to open **Transactions** filtered to that category and its subcategories, for the same period.

#### Hide categories from the widget

<Steps>

1. Click the gear icon on the widget and choose **Exclude categories**.
2. Pick the categories to hide. Hiding a parent also hides its subcategories.
3. Click **Save**. Use **Clear all** to count every category again.

</Steps>

Hidden categories leave both the chart and the total. A badge next to the widget title shows how many are hidden.

The same gear menu has an **Include planned transactions** switch.

### Cash Flow

Shows how much you saved in the period: income minus expenses. Below the headline you'll find a bar comparing the two, then **Income**, **Expenses** and **Saved**. **Saved** is your savings rate, the share of income you kept.

**Previous periods trend** shows the five periods before this one next to it. Click the arrow icon (↗) in the widget's top corner (**View details**) to open the full [Cash Flow report](/stats/analytics-reports/#cash-flow). The arrow is hidden while the widget has no data.

The gear menu has **Exclude categories** and **Include planned transactions**, which work the same way as in **Expenses Structure**.

<Screenshot
  src="/screenshots/stats/cash-flow-widget.png"
  alt="The Cash Flow widget showing net savings, the income and expenses bar, the Income, Expenses and Saved tiles, and the previous periods trend."
  hint="On /dashboard, with the current month selected on a user profile (or demo data) that has both income and expenses this month and in the five months before. Crop to the Cash Flow widget."
/>

<Aside type="note" title="Good to know">
- Categories you set in **Count categories as savings** under [General settings](/settings/general-settings/) are left out of both income and expenses here, so they count as saved.
- A linked refund lowers expenses in the period the money came back. When a whole month is selected, the widget also reads the five months before it, so a refund of a purchase from those months still lowers this month's expenses. Expenses can even go negative. See [Refunds](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/#refunds).
</Aside>

### Categories Watchlist

Keep an eye on the categories you care about. Each row shows what came in minus what went out for that category and its subcategories: green when it's positive, red when it's negative.

- Click an empty **Add category** row to pick categories in **Select Categories**. The widget holds up to 7 categories at size **1×1** and 16 at **1×2**.
- Click the pencil icon to edit the list: drag rows to reorder, click a row to swap its category, or click the bin icon to remove it. Click the save icon when you're done.
- Click a row to open its transactions for the period.
- The gear menu has **Include planned transactions**.
- Each row has a small trend line, which you can turn off with **Show 6-month trend** in the gear menu. It covers the six full months before the month your period ends in, and a higher point means more money moved: more spending on a red row, more income on a green one. The line is hidden when the widget is too narrow.

If you add both a parent category and one of its subcategories, the subcategory's money shows only on its own row, not on the parent's.

### Net Worth

Shows how your net worth changed over the period, including accounts, investments, loans and other assets.

The big number is how much your net worth changed over the period (**End** minus **Start**). Below it:

- **Start**: your net worth going into the period.
- **End**: your net worth at the end of the period.
- **Growth**: the change as a percentage.

You'll also see the change **vs previous** and a **Previous periods trend**. Click the arrow icon (↗) in the widget's top corner (**View history**) to open [Net Worth History](/stats/analytics-reports/#net-worth-history). The arrow is hidden while the widget has no data.

The gear menu opens **Include in net worth**, where you can leave out **Loans**, **Vehicles** or **Ventures**. When something is left out, a badge with a number appears next to the title. Hover it to see what's excluded.

### Credit Utilization

Shows how much of your credit limits you're using right now, per account and in total. It lists active accounts with a credit limit above zero that aren't excluded from statistics. Usage below 40% shows green, 40–69% amber, and 70% or more red. Click an account to open it.

### FIRE progress

<Badge text="Plus" variant="tip" /> A summary of your [FIRE plan](/stats/fire-planner/): how close you are to financial independence and when you could get there. It isn't on a new dashboard. Add it from **Add widgets**, or click **Add to dashboard** on the FIRE planner page, which adds it at **1×1**.

The widget follows the target and assumptions you set in the planner. It shows your progress to the target, **Time to FIRE** with the month (and your age, if you've entered your birth year), and rows for **Saved**, **Next milestone** and your **FIRE number** at your withdrawal rate. At **2×1** it adds your milestones, for example "0 of 4 milestones", from **Today** to your target.

In **Customize** mode, the widget has **1×1** and **2×1** size buttons and a **Style** choice next to them:

- **Ring** (the default): a progress ring with the percentage.
- **Numbers**: a larger time to FIRE with a milestone line. At **2×1**, the milestone line gives way to the **Progress**, **Next milestone** and FIRE number rows and a small chart of your path.

Click the widget, or the arrow icon (↗) in its top corner (**Open FIRE plan**), to open the planner. If the planner doesn't have enough data yet, the widget shows **Set up** instead. Without Plus or a running FIRE planner trial, it shows **Unlock**, which opens the planner.

### Average tax rate

Shows how much of your income goes to taxes: spending in your [tax categories](/settings/general-settings/#tax-categories) divided by your income. Until you choose tax categories, the widget shows **Choose categories**, which opens General settings.

- The big number is the rate over the last 12 months. Next to it are the taxes and income behind it. To average over a shorter span, click the gear icon, open **Averaging period** and pick **Last 3 months** or **Last 6 months**. The widget then also shows how the rate differs from your 12-month rate, in percentage points.
- The bars show your taxes for each of the last 12 months, with the months in the averaging period highlighted. The dashed line is your monthly average (**avg**) over that period. Hover a bar to see that month's taxes and share of income.
- The thin bar at the bottom splits the taxes in the averaging period by category, with each tax category you chose broken down into its subcategories. Up to four parts are shown; the rest are grouped and listed as, for example, "2 more".

The widget always covers the last 12 months and doesn't follow the dashboard's date selector. Click the arrow icon (↗) in the widget's top corner (**View cash flow**) to open the [Cash Flow report](/stats/analytics-reports/#cash-flow).

### Goals

A summary of your [goals](/goals/overview/): each active goal with its progress bar, how much you've saved of the target, its status and its date. Paused goals are listed too. Reached and archived goals aren't. Click a goal to open it, or the arrow icon (↗) in the widget's top corner (**View all**) to open the **Goals** page.

The widget comes in **1×1**, **1×2** and **2×1**. When it's wide enough, goals are laid out in two columns.

Goals read their accounts' balances, so accounts with **Exclude from statistics** turned on still count here. See [How progress is calculated](/goals/how-progress-is-calculated/).

With no active goals, the widget shows **Create a goal**. If your plan doesn't include goals either, it shows **Goals are locked** with a **Learn more** link to the **Goals** page.

### Latest Transactions

Your most recent transactions. The arrow icon (↗) in the widget's top corner (**View all**) opens the full list. In its settings, under **Exclude transactions**, you can hide **Between accounts** transfers, **Out of wallet** transfers, **Balance adjustments** and **Planned transactions** from the list.

**Out of wallet** transfers are hidden by default. Switch that exclusion off to see them.

Balance adjustments are recorded as **Out of wallet** transfers, so they're hidden by default too. To see them, switch off the **Out of wallet** exclusion. **Balance adjustments** then lets you hide only the adjustments while keeping other **Out of wallet** transfers visible.

## Comparing with the previous period

Several widgets compare the period with the one before it, and they don't all define "previous" the same way:

- **Expenses Structure**, **Cash Flow** and **Net Worth**: when you've selected a whole month, the previous calendar month. Otherwise, the same number of days just before.
- **Balance trend**: always the same number of days just before.

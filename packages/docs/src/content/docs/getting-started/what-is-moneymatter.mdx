MoneyMatter is a personal finance app that shows you where your money is and where it goes. You add your accounts and transactions, by hand or synced from your bank, and MoneyMatter turns them into balances, a dashboard and reports in one currency of your choice.

It runs in your browser on a computer or a phone. On a phone, a bar at the bottom has icons for Dashboard, Accounts, Transactions and Analytics, and the menu button opens everything else. You can use the app in English, Ukrainian and several community-translated languages. See [General settings](/settings/general-settings/#language).

## Accounts

- **Manual accounts** for anything you want to track: cash, a current account, a credit card, savings and more. Each one has its own currency and balance, and you record its transactions yourself. See [Create an account](/accounts/create-an-account/).
- **Bank-synced accounts** bring transactions in from your bank automatically. MoneyMatter connects through Monobank, SimpleFIN, Lunch Flow, Enable Banking, Walutomat and Akahu, and self-hosted installs can add Plaid for the USA and Canada. This is part of the [Plus plan](/settings/plans-and-billing/). See [Bank connections](/import/bank-connections/).
- **Account groups** keep related accounts together in the sidebar. See [Account groups](/accounts/account-groups/).
- **Vehicles**, valued automatically so your net worth includes them. See [Vehicles](/accounts/vehicles/).
- **Loans** such as a mortgage or a car loan, with a payoff projection and the payments you've made. See [Loans](/accounts/loans/).
- **Sharing**: give people you trust access to one account, or to all your accounts through a household. See [Household and sharing](/settings/household-and-sharing/).

## Transactions

Every transaction is an **Expense**, an **Income** or a **Transfer**. You can describe each one in as much detail as you like:

- **Categories** with subcategories. New accounts come with a starter set you can edit. See [Categories](/transactions/categories/).
- **Tags**, up to 20 per transaction. See [Tags](/transactions/tags/).
- **Payees**, so you know who you paid. Picking a payee can fill in the category you usually use for it. See [Payees](/transactions/payees/).
- **Splits**, when one payment covers several categories, such as groceries and household items on one receipt. See [Split a transaction](/transactions/split-a-transaction/).
- Notes, locations and file attachments.

You can also bundle related transactions into **Groups** from the Transactions page. See [Transaction groups](/transactions/groups/).

**Automations** are rules for new transactions that come from bank sync and file imports. They can set the category, tags, payee or note, or leave a transaction out of your stats. See [Automations](/transactions/automations/).

## Transfers and refunds

- **Transfers** move money between your own accounts. They don't count as spending or income, because the money is still yours. The same goes for money you move into an investment portfolio or a venture deal. A payment to a loan you track is the exception: it counts as spending.
- **Out of wallet** transfers record money going to, or coming from, somewhere you don't track in MoneyMatter.
- **Refunds** link money you got back to the original purchase, so your spending in that category goes down instead of your income going up.
- **Transfer matching**, under **Transactions** → **Optimizations**, suggests expense and income pairs that look like transfers between your accounts, and links each pair you confirm. See [Link existing transactions](/transfers-and-refunds/link-existing-transactions/).

Read [How transfers work](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/) and [Refunds](/transfers-and-refunds/refunds/) for the details.

## Many currencies, one base currency

Each account keeps its own currency. Your totals, dashboard and reports are all shown in your **base currency**, which you choose when you sign up. Exchange rates update automatically from market rates, and you can set your own rate for any currency if you prefer. See [Base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/).

## Dashboard and analytics

The **Dashboard** is made of widgets you can add, remove and rearrange with **Customize**. They include **Balance trend**, **Expenses structure**, **Cash flow**, **Net worth**, **Latest transactions**, **Subscriptions & bills**, **Planned**, **Budget**, **Goals** and more. See [Dashboard](/stats/dashboard/).

The **Analytics** section has deeper reports:

- **Trends comparison**: compare spending and income with the previous period.
- **Cash flow**: income against expenses over time, and where the money went.
- **Net worth history**: how your assets, liabilities and net worth changed over time.
- **Pivot**: break spending down by any dimension, lay it across time, and compare.
- **Investments**: **Net worth drivers** (how much of your growth you saved, and how much the market returned), **Contributions** (how much cash you've moved into your portfolios over time) and **Investment projection** (how your portfolio could grow with regular contributions).
- **FIRE**: when your savings could cover your spending for good. See [FIRE planner](/stats/fire-planner/).

See [Analytics reports](/stats/analytics-reports/). To understand exactly what goes into these numbers, read [How stats are calculated](/stats/how-stats-are-calculated/).

## Planning

The **Planning** section of the sidebar helps you plan ahead:

- **Budget**: give every line of your spending an amount for each period, and see what's left. See [Start budgeting](/budgeting/start-budgeting/).
- **Goals**: track what you're saving for. See [Goals](#goals) below.
- **Trackers**: collect the transactions for a trip, a project or a year of freelance income, with an optional limit. See [Trackers](/planned/trackers/).
- **Recurring payments**: keep track of subscriptions, bills, installments and regular income, and see what's due next. See [Recurring payments](/planned/recurring-payments/).

You can also add **planned transactions** for money you expect to spend or receive on a future date. See [Planned transactions](/planned/planned-transactions/).

## Goals

**Goals** track what you're saving for, such as a car or an emergency fund. Point a goal at the accounts that hold the money, with a percentage of each, and it shows how much you've saved, your monthly pace, when you'll reach the target and how much you need each month. A **What if** tab lets you try a different plan, and a banner checks whether all your goals fit in what you typically save. Goals are part of the Essential and Plus plans. See [Goals](/goals/overview/).

## Bring in your existing data

Under **Settings** → **Import/Export**, you can import transactions from a CSV file, an OFX or QFX bank statement, or almost any text such as a bank statement or an email. Importing from text uses AI. It's included in the [Plus plan](/settings/plans-and-billing/), and on Essential it needs your own AI model. You can also move over from YNAB, Wallet (BudgetBakers) and Microsoft Money. See [Import from CSV](/import/import-from-csv/) and [Other import sources](/import/other-import-sources/).

The same page lets you export your data to JSON, CSV or Excel files. See [Export your data](/settings/export-data/). You can also download a backup of nearly all your data (attachments and bank connections aren't included). See [Backup and restore](/settings/security-and-account/#backup-and-restore).

## Investments

- **Portfolios** track your holdings, trades, cash and performance, with analytics and a watchlist on Plus. You can import trades from your broker. See [Portfolios](/investments/portfolios/) and [Import investment transactions](/investments/import-transactions/).
- **Venture** tracks private investments, such as SPVs, syndicates and funds, with their events and returns. See [Venture](/investments/venture/).

Both count toward your net worth.

## Also in MoneyMatter

- **AI features**: sort transactions into categories with AI, use your own AI model, and connect AI assistants such as Claude or ChatGPT to your MoneyMatter data. See [AI features](/ai/ai-features/) and [Connect Claude or ChatGPT](/ai/connect-claude-chatgpt-mcp/).
- **iPhone Shortcuts**: let your iPhone log Apple Pay taps, bank SMS and bank notifications for you, on Plus. See [Log payments automatically on iPhone](/iphone/payment-automations/).
- **Open source**: MoneyMatter's code is public, and you can run your own copy. See [Self-host MoneyMatter with Docker](/getting-started/self-hosting/).

## Plans

On moneymatter.app, a new sign-up starts a free 40-day trial that includes almost every feature. The trial is tied to your email address, so signing up again with the same email doesn't start a new one. After that, you choose the **Essential** or **Plus** plan to keep adding to your data. Read [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/) to compare them.

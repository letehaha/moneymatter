This guide takes you from sign-up to your first recorded transaction. It takes a few minutes, and by the end your dashboard will show a real balance.

<Aside type="tip" title="Just want to look around?">
You can explore MoneyMatter with sample data before you sign up. See [Try the demo](#try-the-demo).
</Aside>

## 1. Create your MoneyMatter account

<Steps>

1. Go to [moneymatter.app](https://moneymatter.app) and click **Get Started**.
2. On the **Create an account** page, fill in **Email**, **Display Name**, **Password** and **Verify Password**, then click **Sign up**.
3. You'll see **Check your email**. Open the email titled "Verify your MoneyMatter email" and click **Verify Email**.
4. MoneyMatter signs you in and takes you to the next step.

</Steps>

The page also offers **Sign up with Google** and **Sign up with GitHub**. If you use one of those, there's no email to confirm and you go straight to the next step.

<Aside type="caution" title="Use at least 8 characters for your password">
The form lets you type a 6-character password, but anything shorter than 8 characters is rejected with "Password is too short."
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Add a backup way to sign in">
There's no way to reset a forgotten password. If you signed up with a password, add Google or GitHub sign-in, or a passkey, so you can't get locked out. The dashboard reminds you with **Add a backup login method** and a **Go to Security Settings** button. See [Security and account](/settings/security-and-account/).
</Aside>

### If the email doesn't arrive

- Check your spam folder.
- On the **Check your email** page, click **click here to resend**. You can resend once every 60 seconds.
- Keep the **Check your email** tab open until you've verified. Resending only works from that page, in that tab.
- Click the link within 1 hour. After that it expires.

If you try to sign in before verifying, you'll see "Please verify your email before signing in." Signing in doesn't send a new email. If you've already closed the **Check your email** page, or your link has expired, email support@moneymatter.app and we'll help you verify your account.

## 2. Choose your base currency

Next you'll see **Select Base Currency**. Your base currency is the one every total, chart and report is shown in, so pick the currency you use most. Accounts in other currencies are converted into it.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Base Currency** list. You can type to search, for example "eur".
2. Pick your currency. **USD** is selected by default, so check it before you continue.
3. Click **Confirm Currency**.

</Steps>

You can't skip this step. Every page in the app sends you back here until you've confirmed a currency.

Below the button, you'll also see that your free 40-day trial has started. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing/) for what it includes.

You can change your base currency later in **Settings** → **Currencies**, but MoneyMatter then recalculates every transaction with historical exchange rates. It's best to choose carefully now. See [Base currency and currencies](/settings/base-currency-and-currencies/).

## 3. Find your way around

You land on the **Dashboard**. Until you have an account, it shows a **Welcome to MoneyMatter** card with three ways to start:

- **Create Manual Account**: an account you keep up to date yourself. This guide uses this option.
- **Connect Your Bank**: sync transactions from your bank automatically. This is part of the [Plus plan](/settings/plans-and-billing/), and it's included in your trial. See [Bank connections](/import/bank-connections/).
- **Import from File**: bring in transactions from a bank statement or a CSV file. See [Import from CSV](/import/import-from-csv/).

Around it:

- **The sidebar** on the left has **Dashboard**, **Accounts**, **Transactions** and **Analytics**, plus a list of your accounts and their balances. On a phone, use the bar at the bottom of the screen, or the menu button in the top bar for the full sidebar.
- **The top bar** has the **Add transaction** button (**Add** on a phone), which you can use from any page, and the gear button that opens **Settings**.
- **A banner** at the top shows how many days are left in your trial, with a **See plans** link.

### The Quick Start checklist

Each time you open the app, the **Quick Start** checklist opens beside the page, until you've done every task or dismissed it permanently. It lists things to try, grouped into topics, and shows how many you've done.

- Tasks tick themselves off when you do them. For example, "Create your first account" is ticked when you create one in step 4.
- Click a task you haven't done yet to open the page where you can do it.
- The list includes features beyond the basics, such as trackers and household sharing. Skip anything you don't need.
- To close it for now, click the **X** at its top. On a computer it folds into a narrow **Quick Start** strip on the right edge of the screen, which you click to open it again. On a phone, tap the round button at the bottom right. It opens again the next time you load the app. To stop that, use **Dismiss permanently**.
- To hide it for good, click **Dismiss permanently**, then **Dismiss**. You can turn it back on in **Settings** → **General** with the **Quick Start guide** switch.

## 4. Create your first account

An account is anywhere you keep money: a bank account, a credit card or a cash wallet. Start with the one you use most.

<Steps>

1. On the dashboard, click **Create Manual Account**. You can also open **Accounts** → **All Accounts** in the sidebar and click **Create account**. On a narrow screen, it's in the **⋮** menu next to the **Accounts** title.
2. In **Account name**, type a name you'll recognize, such as "Main Checking".
3. Check the **Currency**. It starts as your base currency. To use a different one, open the list and click **Add new currency +** at the bottom, pick the currency and click **Add**. It's then selected for this account. You can't change an account's currency later, so pick the currency the account actually holds.
4. In **Initial balance**, enter how much is in the account right now. For a credit card you already owe money on, enter a negative number.
5. In **Account Category**, choose the kind of account, such as **Current account**, **Credit card** or **Cash**. If you're not sure, leave it on **General**.
6. For a credit card, enter its **Credit limit**. Otherwise leave it at 0.
7. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

The account's balance starts at its initial balance. From then on, every income and incoming transfer adds to it, and every expense and outgoing transfer takes away from it. See [Create an account](/accounts/create-an-account/) for more on each field.

## 5. Add your first transaction

<Steps>

1. Click **Add transaction** at the top of the page.
2. Keep the **Expense** tab selected for money you spent, or choose **Income** for money you received.
3. Enter the **Amount** as a positive number, for example 12.50.
4. Check the **Account**. It's filled in for you, but make sure it's the right one.
5. Choose a **Category**, for example **Food & Drinks** → **Groceries**. A category is already selected when the dialog opens, so check it.
6. Check the **Datetime**. It starts at the current date and time.
7. Optionally, choose a **Payee**. To add a **Note** or **Tags**, use the **More options** column on the right, or on a phone tap **More options** at the bottom of the dialog.
8. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

The transaction appears in **Transactions** and the account's balance updates right away.

<Aside type="tip" title="Amounts are always positive">
Don't type a minus sign for spending. The **Expense** and **Income** tabs decide whether money goes out or comes in.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Moving money between your own accounts?">
Use the **Transfer** tab instead of recording an expense in one account and an income in the other. A transfer isn't counted as spending or income, because the money is still yours. See [How transfers work](/transfers-and-refunds/how-transfers-work/).
</Aside>

## Try the demo

The demo lets you explore MoneyMatter with sample data, without signing up.

<Steps>

1. Go to [moneymatter.app](https://moneymatter.app).
2. Click **Try Demo**.

</Steps>

You get a temporary account with USD as its base currency, four everyday accounts in USD, EUR and PLN with about three years of transactions, plus sample vehicles, loans, investments, trackers and recurring payments.

- **It lasts 4 hours.** A banner at the top shows the time left. When it runs out, you're signed out, and the demo account and all its data are deleted soon after.
- **It can't become your real account.** Clicking **Sign up** in the banner leaves the demo and starts a new, empty account.
- **Some features are turned off**, such as bank connections, passkeys, password changes, attachments, sharing, backups and data export. Where a feature is turned off, you'll usually see "This feature is not available in demo mode".

## Good to know

- **You start with categories and tags.** New accounts get a set of categories, such as **Food & Drinks** and **Shopping**, and three tags: **Want**, **Need** and **Must**. They're created in the language the app is using when you sign up. Rename or delete them as you like. See [Categories](/transactions/categories/) and [Tags](/transactions/tags/).
- **Help is one click away.** Click your email address (or your initial, if the sidebar is collapsed) at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Help & docs** to open this help center.
